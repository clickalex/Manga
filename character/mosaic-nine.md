# The Mosaic Nine — Lead Character Design Sheets

> The nine leads, one per realm's *face*, designed to be dropped into any medium (manga, anime, movie, TCG, PC game) as a standalone face of the IP. Each sheet: design, on-page palette (hex, for the art team and the card/game UI), motifs & props, pilot anchors, and adaptation notes.
> **Canon:** profiles and pilot lines from [../story/03-characters.md](../story/03-characters.md); realm & sigil system from [../story/02-races-and-realms.md](../story/02-races-and-realms.md) and [../story/04-power-system.md](../story/04-power-system.md); pilot appearances from [../story/manga/](../story/manga/).
> **The Nine-Color Key** (used by the scarves, the record page, and every "nine" graphic in the franchise):

| Realm | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Terrania (Mortal Lands) | copper | `#b87333` |
| Sylvaris (Verdant) | silver-green | `#7fb069` |
| Khazadûm (Stone) | forge-ember | `#e07a3f` |
| Ulgar (The Barrens) | bone-iron | `#9aa5a6` |
| Thalassa (Deep Sea) | deep teal | `#3e8e9e` |
| Emberfall (Infernal) | oath-red | `#d1495b` |
| Seraphel (Sky) | dawn-gold | `#e8b04b` |
| Nocturne (Shadow) | candle-violet | `#7a6a9c` |
| The Seams (In-Between) | white light (all colors at once) | `#f4f4f0` |

> A Seams-colored thing shows *all nine* — that is the whole point (the Seams is where the light enters). The nine-colored scarf, the record page's borders, the Re-Oath hall's banners, the TCG's "Seams" card frame: all nine, in this order.

## 1 · KAELEN VOSS — Human · Mosaic · 16 · Terrania

**The face of the crack.** Protagonist. The only Mosaic of his generation — a piece of the First Being's soul, the world's blank canvas and its load-bearing choice.

**Design.** Lean, blacksmith's hands (callused, one burn on the left thumb he never heals on purpose), messy dark hair, workman's coat too big for him (his father's). The **shifting scar** on his chest — a thin line from collarbone to sternum — is the on-page indicator of which realm he is touching: base **gray-green**, blooming **silver-green** near Sylvaris, **ember** near Khazadûm/Emberfall, **teal** near Thalassa, **gold** near Seraphel (canon: the scar's color is the indicator; Aldric's journal sketch, Ch. 1 P10.4, shows it in five colors — gray, green, ember, teal, gold). The **white threads** at its edge are the *cost* — they lengthen as he spends (tracked Ch. 1 P2.2 → Ch. 5; see the pilot canon list in the manga README).

**Palette.**
- Hair `#2b2b31` · Skin `#e8b88f` · Work coat `#6b5d4f` (soot-brown) · Undershirt `#c9c2b4`
- Scar base `#9aa5a1` · Scar bloom (Sylvaris) `#7fb069` · Scar bloom (Khazadûm) `#e07a3f` · Scar bloom (Thalassa) `#3e8e9e` · Scar bloom (Seraphel) `#e8b04b`
- White threads (the cost) `#f4f4f0`
- **Scar of absence** (Vaelthorn's presence, Ch. 6 only): the scar goes **white** — `#f4f4f0` over the base, the color of *absence*, the one color it has never been.

**Motifs & props.** The hammer (his, not Askstone's — a common smith's hammer, he fixes things with it before he argues) · the **Voss bell** (his father's last work, fist-sized, no ornament — the *mortal note*; it rings when names are returned, and once on its own — the pilot allows it once, Ch. 3 P10.3) · the journal (Aldric Voss's, given by Roderick, Ch. 2 P11.1) · the white threads.

**Pilot anchors.** Ch. 1 P2.2 (first bloom) · Ch. 1 P8.4 (first Name-Return, Raelin) · Ch. 2 P7.2–P8.1 (the grove's vote, the green ring) · Ch. 3 P8.3 ("I'm going to Sylvaris") · Ch. 5 P4 (the great Name-Return, "Arvel") · Ch. 6 P10 (the white scar, the Hollow).

**Pilot line (canon, verbatim — spoken to himself, to a new Mosaic child, and to the audience; three times in the story):** *"You're not a prophecy, kid. You're a person."*

**Adaptation notes.** His art *must* stay ordinary (no eye-glow, no power aura) — the power is the scar and the white threads, both small, both on the chest. TCG: the "Mosaic" frame (white light, all nine). PC game: the cost meter is the white threads; the player *sees* Kaelen fraying.

## 2 · LYRA SYLVARI — High Elf · 17 (elven: ~170 rings) · Sylvaris

**The edge and the conscience.** Last true Everbloom bearer. The elf who can't afford to lose anything, learning to choose the loss.

**Design.** Tall, **silver-green hair worn loose** against court custom (court elves bind theirs — hers flies; an exile's tell, and later, the mark of the woman who chose the grove over the crown), **growth-rings visible at her temples** (her age, drawn — a new ring appears when she spends or chooses: Ch. 2 P8.4, Ch. 5; the reader learns to count them). Everbloom's soft green glow under her cloak (the only *bright* thing on a page when she arrives, Ch. 2 P2.1). From Ch. 2 P9.2 on she wears the **nine-colored scarf** (Odette's knitting) — she hates it, never takes it off (the tsundere, in a scarf).

**Palette.**
- Hair `#b8c4b0` (silver-green) · Skin `#e9d5c3` · Temple rings `#9fb8a8`
- Court cloak `#3f4a44` · Everbloom glow `#a3c98a`
- The scarf (the Nine-Color Key, in realm order): `#b87333` `#7fb069` `#e07a3f` `#9aa5a6` `#3e8e9e` `#d1495b` `#e8b04b` `#7a6a9c` `#f4f4f0`
- White threads (her cost, shown when she says Arvel's name, Ch. 3 P12.1) `#f4f4f0`

**Motifs & props.** The rings (her age, her cost, her currency) · the scarf · the great tree (her cathedral) · the Crown-Touch she *refuses* in the pilot (Ch. 6: "I won't give you my self. I'll give you my *rings*.")

**Pilot anchors.** Ch. 2 P2.1 (arrival) · P2.3 (the pilot line, delivered) · P8.4 (the new ring) · Ch. 3 P9.3 (the private corner — the choosing) · Ch. 5 P1–P4 (Arvel) · Ch. 6 P5 (The First Dawn; the refusal).

**Pilot line (canon, verbatim):** *"I've waited three hundred years for something worth this. Don't waste it being brave."*

**Adaptation notes.** Her wall is a *courtesy* — the panels show the wall going up (composed) and down (one crack) as separate visual states; the lettering never explains them. Her rings are her *health bar*: drawn, countable, spent.

## 3 · GRIMM KHAZDÛRIN — Deep Dwarf · 19 · Khazadûm

**The hammer and the heart.** Forge-knight, heir to a family forge that's been cold a century. Loud, loyal, feeds everyone he meets; grieving a *relative* (the mountain) he can no longer hear.

**Design.** Stocky, **beard in twelve braids** (nine tight — family; three loose, unfinished — debt; the reader is meant to count), stone-scarred hands, the hammer **Askstone** at his hip (his grandfather's, made "to ask the mountain things" — *old* and *polished* the way a family knife is). The silent beat: his hand *pauses* on stone, listening (the stone-song went quiet the day the Reforgers started mining — the art team gives it one silent beat, Ch. 3 P3.2, no dialogue).

**Palette.**
- Beard — family braids `#7a4a2b` · debt braids `#4a3527` (loose, faded)
- Skin `#c98d68` · Forge-knight armor `#5b5f66` · Harness `#6b5d4f`
- Askstone — haft `#5a4632` (polished old wood) · head `#8a8f98` (steel, worn bright)
- Stone-song (when it returns, Vol. 4): a faint `#e07a3f` vein-light in the rock — the **mountain's** money color, held for Vol. 4: at scale, on the surface, as a bloom. *(Audit note, Ch. 13: S1's use of the hue is the under-roads' answering ember-line — first cracked open Ch. 10 P9.2, carried as scene-light through Ch. 11–13: under the floor, one line, never a bloom.)*

**Motifs & props.** The twelve braids (count them) · Askstone · the geode (Pebble's one idea: *somewhere's a geode, and it's his*) · the bridge (he rebuilds what falls — "a door that doesn't hold is just a hole with opinions").

**Pilot anchors.** Ch. 3 P3 (debut, the bridge) · P11.1 (the bridge plan) · Ch. 4 P1 (the bridge *holds*) · Ch. 5 (the first death, beside Kaelen).

**Pilot line (canon, verbatim):** *"My dad's forge's been cold a hundred years. I'm not leaving until it's warm again."*

**Adaptation notes.** His comedy is *feeding people* (he carries extra bread, extra stone, extra everything). His tragedy is one silent beat per chapter — do not over-draw it.

## 4 · MORGATHA ULRIK — Orc · 20 · Ulgar

**The blade and the moral engine.** Exiled blood-clan captain. Honorable, furious, afraid of her own rage — Titanvein means her anger is a fuel line, and she has seen what a full tank does.

**Design.** Massive, **bone-and-iron regalia**, scars written in **oath-script across both forearms** — *legible* (the art team's key detail: a reader who's paid attention can see they're *words*; her body is a library, and every page's a promise). One braid over her shoulder that ends **cut** — the oath she's still keeping (Tarkhan's, her brother's, taken by a Reforgers' cell). The **war-song count**: she counts her breaths in a war-song; the art team prints the count in the corner of her frames (**four… three… two… one…**) — the pilot's hidden clock. *When the count stops, that's the panel.* (Ch. 4 P8: one panel, the cell's method. Ch. 6: the rest — she counts to *zero*, on purpose.)

**Palette.**
- Skin (deep orc green) `#5c6e58` · Oath-script scars `#d8c3a5` (scar-white, *words*)
- Bone regalia `#e8e0cf` · Iron `#5b5f66` · Braid `#3a3f3a`
- The cut end (the unfinished oath): the thread shown *loose*, `#d8c3a5` — never re-knotted in the pilot
- Titanvein (rage, Vol. 5): a faint vein-light `#d1495b` under the skin when she's close — do not use before Vol. 5's Ultravote

**Motifs & props.** The count (the clock) · the cut braid (the debt) · the oath-script (the body as book) · "readable" (her creed: *I intend to stay readable*).

**Pilot anchors.** Ch. 3 P4 (debut) · P4.3 (Lyra meets the code) · Ch. 4 P8 (the count stops) · Ch. 5 P6 (holding the door beside Thornring) · Ch. 6 (the rest).

**Pilot line (canon, verbatim):** *"My body's a library. So far, every page's been a promise. I intend to stay readable."*

**Adaptation notes.** She does *debt*, not warmth — her affection is payment, on the page. Her rage is never drawn as a monster-face (no race is the monster): it's drawn as the *count getting loud* in the corner of the frame.

## 5 · NEREIA THAL — Merfolk · 18 · Thalassa

**The tide and the timer.** Tide-diviner who left her sea because "the first song is gone." The team's clock: out of water, she has hours.

**Design.** Scale-armor, **coral-crown**, **bioluminescent mood-markings** — the art team's recurring tell: *her markings are her subtext; the reader learns to read them before her dialogue* (low tide: quiet, worried, patient — `#7fd1e0`; rising: `#a8f0dc`; attention: a quick ripple pattern). A **water-flask** at her hip, the ticking clock (the art team shows the flask's *level* in every pilot panel with her — it goes down; the reader does the math).

**Palette.**
- Skin `#cfe3de` · Scale-armor `#3e8e9e` (deep teal) over `#2c5d6e` · Coral-crown `#e76f51`
- Mood-markings — low tide `#7fd1e0` · rising `#a8f0dc` · attention `#d8f3ef`
- Water-flask `#9ad3d8` (the water, visible level) · Her voice (drawn): faint ripple-lines, no words, `#7fd1e0`

**Motifs & props.** The flask (the clock) · the markings (the subtext) · the first line (the song she carries — the sea's first law; it stopped reaching the surface a year ago) · "I'm not drowning, I'm *on a deadline*" (her humor, canon voice).

**Pilot anchors.** Ch. 3 P6 (debut, the first line) · P6.2 (the bell answers the sea) · Ch. 4 (the water's edge, against the white) · Ch. 6 (her first line *inside* the Chorus-True fragment — the S2 Thalassa seed, planted).

**Pilot line (canon, verbatim):** *"My people's law is a song. Right now, the song's out of water. So am I. I think we can help each other."*

**Adaptation notes.** She is *read* before she is *heard* — every frame with her, the markings change first, the dialogue second. The flask level is a drawn countdown; the reader's tension is arithmetic.

## 6 · AZRAEL VENN — Fallen Angel · 22 · Seraphel

**The ace and the wound.** Strongest fighter; least sure he's allowed to want anything. Dry, over-precise, deflects with jokes that are technically not jokes; the calmest person alive — because he has already lost the fight that matters.

**Design.** **Gray wings** — the same gray as the Unraveling (the art team's subtlest visual argument: the fallen angel's wings are the color of the thing that eats names, and they are *the most human color on the page*; the reader must *not* read them as the gray) with **dawn-pink wing-tips** (the light didn't go out; it *chose people over law*). The **halo is a cracked statute** (the crack runs through the halo *like a river* — the oath he broke: *"I will protect no mortal from the Purge."* He broke it. He hid the in-between children. The Wandering Order.). Pale, precise, the calmest face in the cast.

**Palette.**
- Wings — gray `#6d6e71` · wing-tips `#f7a8b8` (dawn-pink)
- Halo — light `#f5e9d0` · the crack (river) `#9aa5a6`
- Skin `#ead9c9` · Fugitive's coat `#4a4a52` · His eyes: no color on the page (the art team withholds them until Vol. 8's trial — the one character whose eyes are never drawn in the pilot; the reader's held breath, made ink)
- The Wing-Broken (his Chord with Bramble, seeded Ch. 6, played Vol. 8): the gray wings in the Chord's *new hue* (rain-on-new-leaves teal-green) — do not use before the seed, do not draw it full until Vol. 8

**Motifs & props.** The crack (the oath, visible in the light) · the hidden children (the Wandering Order — Bramble among them) · the pending trial (he is *waiting* for the angels; the Re-Oath is a trial, and he shows up at trials) · "the falling is ongoing" (his mentor's line, canon 03a: the fall is a *walk*).

**Pilot anchors.** Ch. 6 P8 (the sky panel — the pilot's first sky; his pilot line; he *stops* when he sees Bramble's mask).

**Pilot line (canon, verbatim):** *"I broke one oath and they made me saint for it. The trick now is being a person, and I find that harder."*

**Adaptation notes.** He is the cast's *calm* — every panel with him, the lettering slows one size. His eyes are withheld (the art team: no iris color in the pilot — the reader meets the calmest face in the story and cannot find his eyes; Vol. 8's trial is the reveal). His wings are *gray on purpose* — protect the ambiguity.

## 7 · VIVIENNE "VIV" NOX — Vampire · 15 (looks) / unknown (actually) · Nocturne

**The scholar and the spark.** Vampire prodigy who carries an umbrella and sunscreen like armor. Sharp, curious, *happier than she's allowed to be* — the dead's society runs on restraint; Viv runs on questions.

**Design.** Pale, **candle-gold eyes**, a **Regency pin** in her hair — worn *on purpose*, the find-me pin (if her Council wants to find her, they can; the treason pin, the art team's tell). A tailored coat, a **folded umbrella** under one arm (it is *sunny* — a vampire in the sun, and she is *out* in it; the pilot's first visual joke of the Nocturne realm), a **sunscreen vial** in the other hand (the first lie, named: *the Council says the sun burns. It doesn't burn.* She is *slightly late to the sun* — a small rebellion, and it's *hers*). Fights with a **library** — she quotes law at you until you're embarrassed to be a villain.

**Palette.**
- Skin `#f0e6dc` · Eyes (candle-gold) `#f5d76e` · Hair `#3b3630` (dark, the Regency pin against it)
- Regency pin `#b87333` (copper — the find-me pin, the one warm thing she wears)
- Coat `#3b3630` · Umbrella `#2b2b31` (black, closed, *held like a sword she's declined to use*) · Sunscreen vial `#f5d76e` (the gold, the sun, in a bottle)
- Veilward (her Sigil, drawn): a faint after-image behind her — a *double* of her, one frame late, in candle-violet `#7a6a9c` (memory-shadow; the art team: the double is always *one step behind*, never ahead — the dead's manners, made visual)

**Motifs & props.** The umbrella (the sun, declined and taken) · the Regency pin (the find-me pin) · the library (the weapon) · "slightly late to the sun" (the rebellion).

**Pilot anchors.** Ch. 3 P5 (debut, mid-sentence) · P5.3 (the one panel where the vampire is in full sun with the umbrella *down*) · Ch. 4 P1–P2 (quoting the gray's *violation* into the record — the lawyer at the apocalypse) · Ch. 6 (the record, with Vaelis).

**Pilot line (canon, verbatim):** *"My kind's been dead a thousand years and still we're* polite. *It's the only horror story I can think of that's funnier than it should be."* — (The law is her *weapon*; the sun is her *grief* — the pilot spends the joke, the season spends the sun.)

**Adaptation notes.** She is *always* mid-sentence (the reader joins her argument in progress — the lettering starts every frame with an em-dash). Her double is one frame *late*, never ahead. The umbrella is held like a sword she's declined to use — the pilot's quietest threat.

## 8 · BRAMBLE — Changeling · ~14 · The Seams

**The scout and the heart.** Child of no realm; the first person in the story to see Kaelen as a *person*, not a prophecy. Feral, affectionate, tells the truth at *bad* times — the team's emotional barometer: **when Bramble stops being funny, a character is about to be hurt.**

**Design.** Small, quick, **never the same face twice** (Mimicveil — borrowed shape, found self; the art team: her *default* face — the one the reader learns — is a freckled seam-child face; every other face is a *rental*, drawn with one visible seam-line at the jaw, so the reader can tell the difference). By default she wears **a mask** (Oni-culture: a face is a door; the mask is the door *labeled* — the label is a small worn tag, its writing *illegible to the reader*; the name on the label is the one thing the page withholds — the finale's answer: the name isn't found, it's *given back by the people who know you*, nine voices, each a part). Carries a **tengu news-blade** she's not allowed to use yet (the art team: it's too big for her, and she carries it *carefully* — the not-yet, made prop).

**Palette.**
- Default face — skin `#e8b88f`, freckles `#c98d68`, hair `#4a3a2c` (quick, wind-knotted)
- The rentals (the borrowed faces): the same base with one visible seam-line `#9aa5a6` at the jaw — *never* two seam-lines (the art team's rule: one seam is a face; two is a warning — the first two-seam frame in the saga is her *almost* unraveling, Vol. 10, Seams)
- The mask — worn wood `#a58a6f` · the label (tag) `#e8e0cf`, writing *illegible* (the withholds) · the tie `#6b5d4f`
- The tengu news-blade `#8a8f98` (too big, carried carefully)
- Mimicveil (her Sigil, drawn): when she borrows a shape, the *old* shape lingers half a frame (a ghost-outline, `#f4f4f0`, dissolving — the borrowed, leaving)

**Motifs & props.** The mask (the labeled door) · the label (the withheld name) · the news-blade (the not-yet) · the scarf three sizes too big (Marta's knitting, sent without knowing she'd be there — the hearth's thread, Ch. 6 P12, the last joke, played straight).

**Pilot anchors.** Ch. 6 P8 (debut, out of Marginalia's cart — the reveal) · P8 (her pilot line, to Kaelen, without turning around) · P8 (Azrael: *"I know that mask. I know the shape under it."*) · P12 (the scarf three sizes too big).

**Pilot line (canon, verbatim):** *"You're scared of being the prophecy. I get it. I was scared of being any of the faces. Turns out the face was the easy part."*

**Adaptation notes.** She is the cast's *barometer* — when her humor drops out of a panel, the reader knows a character is about to be hurt (the lettering team: her funny frames get the SFX; her serious frames get *nothing*). Her face is a *system*: default (learned), rental (one seam), warning (two seams). The label's writing is withheld for the whole saga — the TCG and the game must keep the tag *illegible* too.

## 9 · OLD FENN THISTLEDOWN — Gnome · 312 · The Seams

**The mentor and the map.** 300-year-old rift-tinker, last cartographer of the Nine Realms. Cranky, exact, keeps a hundred maps going at once (the world is *unfinished*, and he is the only person who will admit it). The story's narrator-by-absence: the reader's eye in the cart.

**Design.** Goggle-stained, one eye a **rift-glass** (the Unraveling took the other — he *let* it, to see the edge of it better; the art team: the rift-glass eye is the only *bright* thing on him — a glass iris `#7fd1e0` with a crack of light inside, and it *shows the edge of things*: when it's on, the background of the panel is *sketched* — the world as a map, the reader's reward, on the page). Rides **Marginalia**, a cart that is **40% cart, 60% argument** (it talks back; its name is a *margin note*; it has a **plate** at the table — real bread; it is a *witness*, not a chair).

**Palette.**
- Goggles `#8a8f98` · Goggle-stain `#6b5d4f` · Coat (three hundred years of road) `#6b5d4f` over `#5b5f66`
- Rift-glass eye — glass `#7fd1e0` · the crack of light `#f4f4f0` · (the *sketch* state: the panel's background goes line-art when the eye is on — the reader's reward, used sparingly, max 2× per chapter)
- Marginalia (the cart) — wood `#a58a6f` · iron `#5b5f66` · the plate (real bread) `#e8d5b5` · its voice: *tiny italics* (the lettering team: Marginalia's dialogue is always in small italics, always in the *margin* of the frame — a margin note, on the page)
- The map (the silk) `#e8e0cf` · the gray stains (the Unraveling, on the map) `#9aa5a6` → `#6d6e71` (the darker the stain, the closer the edge)

**Motifs & props.** The map (the world, unfinished) · the rift-glass eye (the edge, seen) · Marginalia (the argument, the witness, the friend) · the hand-written captions (the saga's recurring close: *They walked north. The gray watched.* — always in Fenn's hand, bottom right).

**Pilot anchors.** Ch. 1 P9 (the cart arrives — *"Pack a bag."*) · Ch. 2 P1.3 ("the world's got a ten-thing, kid… that's a hungry sign") · Ch. 3 P9.1 (the red pins, named: *Azrael. Bramble.*) · Ch. 6 P10 (the last caption: *The Hollow doesn't chase. It waits. The map knows. The map has always known.*)

**Pilot line (canon, verbatim — delivered Ch. 1 P12.2, the first time he says it out loud):** *"I've mapped nine realms for three hundred years. The world's got a* tenth *and it's been* quiet. *That's never a good sign, kid. That's a* hungry *sign."*

**Adaptation notes.** He is the reader's *hand* — his captions are the book's spine (always bottom right, always his hand, always the last word on the page). Marginalia is a *character* (the lettering team: tiny italics, in the margin, always); its three-hundred-year argument with Pebble and Viv is the pilot's running gag (the gag is *continuity*: the same three lines, in different orders, each time).

## The Nine at the table (the composition the art team must learn)

The **chorus panel** (a ring of one-frame-per-voice, table-centered — Ch. 3 P8.2, P9.2, P11.2) is the team's visual identity and is used *only* for the Nine. The seating (Ch. 3 P8.1, carried through the pilot): Kaelen (center, the fulcrum) · Lyra (his left, the wall) · Fenn + Marginalia's plate (the cart's seat, a half-frame of wheel) · Roderick (the captain's seat — family, at the family table) · Grimm (Pebble on his shoulder) · Morgatha (the count in the corner of her frame) · Viv (the em-dash, always) · Nereia (the flask, the level visible) · **two empty chairs, plates set, a loaf still steaming in each** — until Ch. 6, when Azrael (the sky) and Bramble (the cart) take them, and the table is *full* (eleven: the Nine + the captain + the cart — and the two empty chairs, filled; the pilot's promise, kept in bread).
