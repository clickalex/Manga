**Casting logic** (why the world scales to any medium): every lead is the emotional "face" of a different realm & race, so a manga, an anime, a movie, a TCG, or a PC game can put a different lead front-and-center and the world still holds. The **Mosaic Nine** are the team; the **49 races** are the team's *room*; the 625 names are the room's *furniture*.

**Rules:**
- Any Tier-4 row can be promoted to Tier-3 (give it a function + beat) when a story needs it; any Tier-3 to Tier-2 (give it a profile). The world grows *from the population up*.
- Every lead has: a grief, a weapon, a contradiction, and a line they could say in a pilot. New characters must be built to the same four-part rule before they're added.
- **No race is a monster** (spine #8): a character sheet may never make a race the scary one. The scary one is the *hunger* (the Unraveling / Vaelthorn feeding) and the *worst impulse* each realm already has (the Reforgers).
