## 1. Structure & script integrity — PASS

- **33 chapters × 12 pages = 396 scripted pages.** Every chapter file has exactly 12 `## Page` blocks.
- **Ch. 14–33 carry 24 panels each** (`### P#.#`), 2 per page; Ch. 1–13 are the pilot-format scripts (25–39 beats, sanctioned).
- **Panel numbering is clean per page** (`P1.1, P1.2, P2.1 …`), headers match the file's chapter number, and every `### P` id is unique within its chapter.
- **No Cyrillic or stray non-Latin characters** anywhere in `story/`, and no drafting artifacts (`— no:`, `hmm`, `TODO`, doubled words).
- **END OF CHAPTER lines present** in every chapter; the two prose codas (Ch. 6, Ch. 21) are the sanctioned exceptions.
