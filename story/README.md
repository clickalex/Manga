# AURELION — The Nine Realms Saga

**A cross-media fantasy IP: one world, nine realms, forty-nine races, built from day one for manga, anime, movies, card games, and PC games.**

---

## Logline

> In a world shattered into nine realms — each home to its own civilization and its own kind of being — a scarred human boy, a rare **Mosaic** born with a fragment of the First Being's soul, sets out with one warrior of every realm to stop a hollow god who would devour all races and reforg them into a single painless unity.

## Elevator pitch

*One Piece* meets *Final Fantasy* meets *The World Ends with You*: a multi-race epic where **every race is a civilization, not a monster** — humans, elves, dwarves, orcs, demons, angels, merfolk, undead, beastkin, chimeras and dozens more — united by a simple, emotional question:

> **Can a thousand different worlds stay themselves *and* become one? Unity without erasure.**

## Why this story works (the franchise math)

| Franchise requirement | How AURELION answers it |
|---|---|
| A world big enough to never run out of stories | 9 realms × 49 races × 1 seam-town = hundreds of natural story locations |
| Characters that outlive the source material | 9 leads, each the emotional "face" of a different realm & race — every adaptation can feature a different lead |
| Built-in card game mechanics | The power system (Sigils, Chords, the Unraveling) is *designed as a card system before it is a plot* |
| Built-in game variety | 9 realms = 9 playable race/class families in any PC game; the Mosaic mechanic = a hybrid build class |
| Seasonable anime structure | Each realm gets its own full arc → 3 seasons + movies map 1:1 to the story |
| A theme that survives 10 years | "Unity without erasure" is a theme that re-frames as the sequel, spin-offs, and games expand the world |

## The world in one paragraph

A thousand years ago, **Aurelion** — the First Dreaming, the being that was "everything that has ever been or ever will be" — shattered itself into the world of **Vael** and nine **Crown Shards**. Each shard sank into a realm and became the soul-mark (**Sigil**) of the beings born there. Nine civilizations rose: humans, elves, dwarves, orcs, sea-folk, demons, angels, the dead, and the in-between folk of the Seams. The realms were sealed apart by the Veil, and the Covenant of Nine Crowns held. Now the Veil is collapsing, the realms are decaying, and the **Hollow Sovereign** — Aurelion's own negative image, the shadow left behind when the First Dreaming broke — has awakened to perform the **Reforging**: devour all realms and reforge every race into one. Only a Mosaic can walk the seams between worlds. Kaelen is one.
