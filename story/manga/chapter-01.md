# AURELION · Volume 1 — Chapter 1: "The Bell in the Rain"

> **Pilot · Pages 1–12 · Season 1, Arc A: "The Town"**
> **Cast:** Kaelen, Senna, Ben, Roderick, Warden Gable, Marta, Hilda, Odette, Kessa, Brother Aldric, Pippin, Milo, Raelin (elven soldier), Old Tom, Fenn, Marginalia
> **Locations:** Ashenmere — forge row, the Crossing, the market, Covenant temple, the border grove (silhouette)
> **Arc beat:** Establish the town, the family, the omen → the Riftfall → the first Sigil borrow → Fenn arrives → the hook.
> **Motifs planted:** the bell (mortal note), the nine-colored scarf, the gray thread, the name.

---

## Page 1 — *Ashenmere*

### P1.1 — Wide establishing, top ⅔ of page
**Art:** Ashenmere in morning rain, seen from above the temple hill. A Seam town: the Veil-water runs under the main road like a pale river (it shimmers faintly silver where the Veil is close to the surface). Rooflines of two styles meet mid-town — human timber on one side, elven stone-and-leaf on the other. Laundry lines strung with thread in **nine colors**. A ferry crosses the Sylvaport crossing.
**Caption box:** *Ashenmere. A town built on the line between two worlds. The Veil runs under the road like a river. Most days, you don't notice.*

### P1.2 — Market in the rain
**Art:** Odette's stall (halfling market stall, warm browns). Rain-sparkling. On the stall's edge: a small scarf knitted in **nine colors**, no buyer. Behind it, goblin couriers (three-armed, many-pocketed) weave between customers at inhuman speed.
**Odette (sign on stall):** "MOSAIC SCARF — nine colors, no refunds, no questions."

### P1.3 — The Crossing
**Art:** The ferry (Osric's, old wood, steady). Halfling crates aboard. A goblin courier sprints the wet flagstones ahead of the boat, parcels strapped everywhere.
**Goblin (laughing, to no one in particular):** "The goblin delivers."
**SFX:** *slap-slap-slap* (feet on wet stone)

## Page 2 — *The Forge*

### P2.1 — Wide interior
**Art:** The Voss forge: iron grey walls, ember warmth, tools hung by hand. **KAELEN (16)** at the anvil — lean, blacksmith's hands, messy dark hair, shirt rolled up. **BEN**, his fellow apprentice, banks the fire. On the wall above the anvil, in a wooden case: a small **bell** (Aldric's last work — simple, no ornament, the size of a fist).
**Ben:** "Dad used to say the hammer's a question."
**Kaelen:** "He said a lot of things. The anvil said no."
**SFX:** *CLANG*

### P2.2 — Close on Kaelen's chest (under unbuttoned shirt)
**Art:** A faint scar on his chest — currently **gray**, nearly invisible, like a smudge of un-ink. A single tiny white thread at its edge (easy to miss; reader should find it on re-read).
**Kaelen (thought):** *Gray today. Gray's fine. Gray's just… not yet.*

### P2.3 — The bell rings
**Art:** The town bell (temple tower, off-page) begins to toll — morning muster. Kaelen's hammer **stops mid-swing**. His eyes close for one frame. The ember light in the forge seems to dim in respect.
**SFX:** *doooong.*
**Ben (quiet, respecting it):** "…Five years."

### P2.4 — Senna arrives with food
**Art:** **SENNA (12)** at the door, wrapped in a coat, a wrapped parcel in one hand, a pencil behind her ear, a festival sign in the other (hand-painted: "OPEN DOORS").
**Senna:** "School wants a name for the festival hall. I told them *Open Doors.* They asked why."
**Kaelen (wiping his hands, not turning):** "And?"
**Senna:** "I said — because Dad said a door's a question, not a wall."
**Kaelen (half-smile, finally turns):** "That's a very old joke for a twelve-year-old."
**Senna:** "It's five years old. I'm keeping it."

## Page 3 — *The Keep — the Omen*

### P3.1 — Interior, keep tower
**Art:** **CAPTAIN RODERICK VANE** (Seam-guard captain, weathered, the old comrade's eyes) at the **rift-glass** (a lens of stabilized rift-stone; it shows the Veil-water's deep current). **WARDEN GABLE** behind him. In the glass: the Veil-current, pale silver — and **one thin gray thread** in it, like a hair in milk.
**Gable:** "Captain. It's in the glass again."
**Roderick (long beat, hand on the lens):** "Log it. Don't tell the town."
**Gable:** "…Not yet?"
**Roderick (quieter):** "Not yet."

### P3.2 — Insert panel (dark, one frame, no color)
**Art:** A flashback frame, single-color ink: a doorway, a man holding it shut against white light, five years ago. No faces.
**Roderick (thought, small letters):** *I was outside the door. I watched.*

### P3.3 — On the keep shelf
**Art:** Roderick's hand resting on a small wooden case (the same wood as the forge's bell case). He doesn't open it.
**Caption box:** *Two cases. One town. One family. One name between them.*

## Page 4 — *Town Montage — Festival of Open Doors*

### P4.1 — Marta's bakery
**Art:** **MARTA VELL** flour-dusted, smiling, loaves going out the door; **BRIGID O'MARA** hauling trays; **MILLO** (10) bouncing on a crate watching. Outside, every household's doorstep shows a plate set out with bread (the Festival of Open Doors — every house feeds a stranger once a year).
**Marta (to the line of townspeople):** "A town's fed by its baker and its rumors. Today both are busy."

### P4.2 — Covenant temple
**Art:** **BROTHER ALDRIC** (warm, kind, wrong-about-things-but-not-mean) hanging lanterns with **DEACON ANSELM**; inside, the lantern wall begins — one lantern per household.
**Aldric:** "Open Doors. Feed a stranger. Remember a name. In that order."
**Anselm:** "…I think 'remember a name' is the hard one."
**Aldric (not looking up, smiling):** "That's the whole point."

### P4.3 — The street (gag beat)
**Art:** Milo chasing the twin foxline beastkin scouts **RYN** and **VIX** through the rain, a market basket flying; **HILDA** (town scribe) at her desk under an umbrella, 400 pages of petitions fanned around her, completely unbothered, writing.
**Hilda (to no one):** "The Re-Oath petition needs four hundred pages. I'm on forty."
**SFX:** *skritch*

## Page 5 — *The Market — Pippin*

### P5.1 — Market crowd, tension
**Art:** **PIPPIN ALDOUS** (teen, human-kin — a visible faint **open-mark** on his left arm, a borrowed Sigil that glows weakly, pale green-gold) hanging by the well. A ring of older kids, faces mean but not violent (the prejudice is small and daily, which is worse).
**Kid 1:** "Marked."
**Kid 2:** "Your arm *glows*, Pippin. That's not a fashion."
**Pippin (head down, quiet):** "It's a signature."

### P5.2 — Senna steps in
**Art:** Senna's coat whips into frame, shoulder checking the circle open (she is *small* and *feral* about it).
**Senna:** "He's in my class. Try again."

### P5.3 — Kaelen arrives
**Art:** Kaelen at Senna's shoulder (he heard the bell of his own making — no, he heard Senna's voice; keep it human). He crouches to Pippin's eye level. The kids back off — not out of fear, out of the specific awkwardness of being caught.
**Kaelen (to Pippin, quiet, face to face):** "A mark's not a stain. It's a signature. Someone in this room taught me that."
**Pippin (small):** "…Who?"
**Kaelen:** "My dad."
**Insert — close on Pippin's arm:** the open-mark pulses once, soft, like a heartbeat. *Once.*
**Caption box (small, bottom corner):** *The world's forgetting its names. In Ashenmere, one boy's learning to say them back.*

## Page 6 — *Evening — the Lanterns*

### P6.1 — The temple hill, lantern wall
**Art:** Dusk. The lantern wall blinks alive — a hundred small lights. The town walks up the hill in small clusters, rain stopped, first stars out. It's the warmest page of the chapter.
**SFX:** *hush-hush-hush* (a hundred footsteps)

### P6.2 — Kaelen and Senna under the temple bell
**Art:** The two of them, shoulders almost touching, looking up at the bell. Their reflections in the bell's bronze: two people, one scar (faint), one margin (Senna's pencil in hand, scribbling).
**Senna:** "Dad's people say the realms are one dream."
**Kaelen:** "His people say a lot of things."
**Senna:** "Dad said it like it was *true*."
**Kaelen (long beat):** "…Yeah. He did."

### P6.3 — The first tremor (sub-panel, bottom strip, three small frames)
**Art:** Three tiny frames: (a) a lantern flame bends *sideways* (no wind); (b) the Veil-water under the road shivers — ripples with no source; (c) Kaelen's hand on the bell — he feels it before he hears it. No SFX. **Silence.**
**Kaelen (thought):** *Did you feel that?*

## Page 7 — *The Sky Tears*

### P7.1 — FULL PAGE: white
**Art:** A single white panel. No lines. Nothing. (The tearing is too big to draw.)
**SFX (centered, letters spaced far apart):** *tssssssssss*

### P7.2 — Top ½ page: the rift
**Art:** Above the Sylvaport crossing, the sky is a wound — a ragged line of pale light, like paper torn in a storm. Debris falls *up and down at once* (the Rift doesn't obey the town's physics): roof tiles, a market awning, a whole elven street-lantern, trees from the border grove — and **one figure in elven court armor**, falling straight, *too straight*, as if aimed.
**SFX:** *NNNNNNG* (the alarm bell, Gable's, off-panel)
**Gable (off-panel):** "SEAM GUARD — CROSSING CLOSED! EVERYONE BACK FROM THE WATER!"

### P7.3 — Bottom ½: the landing
**Art:** The elven soldier (**RAELIN**) pinned under the market gate's crossbar, mid-square. Around his armor: **gray threads** — thin, pale, wrong — already crawling out of the rift-light and *up his arm*. He's not screaming. That's the horror. He's looking at the threads like they're a letter he's reading.
**Raelin (whisper):** "…I remember my name."
**Insert — tiny, bottom corner:** the threads *pause* — as if listening.

## Page 8 — *Kaelen Crosses*

### P8.1 — The crowd parts (the first Name-Return, by accident)
**Art:** Kaelen pushing through the frozen crowd (hands, coats, Marta's arm trying to stop him). His face is not heroic. It's the face of a blacksmith deciding a job. **A small frame at the crowd's edge:** MILO (10, the baker's boy) at the crossing's edge — his feet **three inches off the wet flagstones**, pulled toward the Veil-water under the road (the rift's pull — for one second, the world is *forgetting his weight*). Kaelen, mid-push, shouts his name **without looking back**. And the boy *stops*. One frame. No explanation. Kaelen doesn't see it. (The first Name-Return — by accident, on a human, at no cost the reader can see: the reader learns the rule *before* the cost. Canon: Milo is "the first person Kaelen saves by name.")
**Marta (off-panel, pulling at his sleeve):** "Kaelen — *Kaelen*!"
**Kaelen (not stopping):** "Ma, hold her. (Senna.) Don't let go of her hand."
**Kaelen (shouting, without looking back, the first line of the chapter that costs nothing):** "**MILO!**"
**SFX:** *(none — for one frame, the world just listens)*

### P8.2 — The threads turn toward him
**Art:** Kaelen kneeling by Raelin's face. The gray threads slow their crawl up the elf's arm — and *turn*. All of them. Pointing at Kaelen's chest. The white thread at the edge of his scar (from P2.2) is *moving*.
**Raelin (one-word, gasping):** "Cold."

### P8.3 — CLOSE (3/4 page): the bloom
**Art:** Kaelen's chest — the gray smudge of his scar **blooms**: silver-green rings spreading from the center like a tree's growth-ring, like an elven mark being *written onto him in real time*. The light is quiet. The rain stops falling *on him* (a small halo of dry air around him — a first, subtle, rule-bending grace he doesn't understand yet).
**SFX:** *(none. Silence.)*
**Kaelen (thought, small letters):** *…Oh. That's me. That's the* me *part.*

### P8.4 — The first Name-Return
**Art:** Kaelen grips Raelin's collar-plate. His eyes are open, focused, *human*. Raelin's tabard is legible to the reader: a name-plate.
**Kaelen:** "I don't know your rank. I don't know your century. But your name's on your tabard."
**Kaelen (louder, into the elf's eyes):** "You are **Raelin**. You are not the gray. You are Raelin."
**Insert — the threads:** they *stop*. Then — slowly, like a tide going out — they **retreat** from the elf's arm. Not gone: *pushed back*, to the wound in his shoulder. (An honest, partial win.)

### P8.5 — Raelin's eyes clear
**Art:** Raelin's eyes focus — the elf's ring-marked temples visible. He is alive, half-gray, and *oriented*.
**Raelin (breathing, lucid, one sentence):** "…The grove. The grove is voting."
**Kaelen:** "What?"
**Raelin (already fading back into a daze, hand touching the elf's own chest):** "It's… lost the vote. I was the page. I was to tell her. *Lyra was to tell her.*"

## Page 9 — *The Town Reacts*

### P9.1 — Whispers
**Art:** The crowd, 20 feet back, faces: fear, awe, hunger, prayer. A mother pulls a child behind her. Hilda is already writing (the scribe is *always* writing).
**Townswoman 1 (whisper):** "A Mosaic."
**Townswoman 2:** "That's not a word we use."
**Townswoman 1:** "*That's* why we don't use it."

### P9.2 — Odette's stall (motif)
**Art:** Odette has pulled the **nine-colored scarf** down, under the stall, covering it with a basket. She's looking at Kaelen's chest (the bloom is fading to a dull green line). Her expression: not fear — *recognition*.
**Odette (to herself):** "No one's brave enough to wear nine colors."
**Insert — tiny:** she doesn't put the scarf back on the stall. She folds it. *Keeps* it.

### P9.3 — Kaelen's arm (the cost)
**Art:** Close on Kaelen's hand, still gripping Raelin's collar — trembling. And at the edge of his chest-scar (over his shoulder, visible in the frame): **one new white thread**, fine as spider-silk, creeping toward his collarbone. His own mark, fraying. He's pulling his hand back to look.
**Kaelen (thought, not panic — *accounting*):** *I did that. And it cost me a thread.*
**SFX:** *(the rain starts again — the halo's gone. The world resumed.)*

## Page 10 — *Night — The Forge — What He Is*

### P10.1 — Roderick at the forge door
**Art:** Night. Roderick in the rain, holding his coat closed. Kaelen still in his forge clothes, face half-lit by the dying embers. Senna between them, smaller than the two of them, holding a lantern like a weapon.
**Roderick:** "Your father kept a journal. I was the only one he showed it to."

### P10.2 — The journal (insert panel)
**Art:** Aldric's journal page: a sketch of a **shifting scar** (the same scar, in five colors — gray, green, ember, teal, gold), with the blacksmith's cramped handwriting around it.
**Journal text (Aldric's hand):** *"The realms are one dream. A Mosaic is the dream remembering itself. If my son is that — tell him the crack isn't the fault. The crack is where the light was going to go."*

### P10.3 — The answer
**Art:** Three close-ups, vertical strip: (a) Kaelen's face — "What am I?"; (b) Roderick's face — the guilt and the love in the same eyes; (c) Senna's face — the lantern in her hand, steady.
**Kaelen:** "What am I?"
**Roderick:** "A name."
**Roderick (quieter, to Senna, then to Kaelen):** "The temple calls him an omen. The guard calls him a threat. I call him the reason I'm allowed to sleep."
**Roderick (last, flat, blacksmith-to-blacksmith):** "The world's forgetting its names, kid. And you say them back."

## Page 11 — *Dawn — The Cart*

### P11.1 — The Crossing, dawn
**Art:** The crossing is still cordoned (Seam-guard ropes, wet). In the pale dawn: a cart approaches — **40% cart, 60% argument**: mismatched wheels, a dozen small mechanisms, an eye-level telescope, a flag that reads "MARGINALIA" in letters that keep trying to re-arrange themselves. In the driver's seat: **OLD FENN (312)** — goggles, one **rift-glass eye** (it shows the Veil-current like Roderick's lens), a grin that is *not* friendly.
**Fenn (to no one, loud, to the whole town):** "If this town's Mosaic is the one with the hammer hands — tell him I'm here to argue with his map!"
**Marginalia (small italic lettering, from the cart, in a voice like a small angry radio):** "I am not a cart. I am a moving debate."

### P11.2 — The handshake
**Art:** Fenn steps down, looks at Kaelen — and his **rift-glass eye focuses** on the chest-scar (now a dull green line with one white thread). He is *still* for a full beat. The grin goes quiet.
**Fenn (quieter, no longer performing):** "Huh. Three hundred years I've been mapping how the world forgets itself."
**Fenn:** "…And it sends me a boy who remembers."

### P11.3 — Senna's question
**Art:** Senna, lantern held up, eye-level with Fenn (he's *small* — gnome height; the reader finally learns).
**Senna:** "Who are you?"
**Fenn (three fast answers, one per hand, each hand a different gesture):** "A tinkerer." / "A cartographer." / "A grump."
**Fenn (sitting on the cart's edge, uninvited, already at home):** "Pick your favorite. I have opinions about all three."
**Marginalia (small italic):** "Opinions, yes. *Feelings* would imply a schedule."

## Page 12 — *The Map — End of Chapter*

### P12.1 — The map (2/3 page)
**Art:** Fenn's map on the cart's table, unrolled under a lantern: **Vael** — one world shaped like a **nine-pointed crown**, eight realms ringing a center (the Seams, drawn in patchwork colors, the only part of the map with *more* colors than the rest). At the edges of the realms: **gray stains**, thin, creeping — the Unraveling. The Terrania–Sylvaris border (this town) is marked with a small black dot.
**Kaelen (pointing):** "That's us. That's the dot."
**Fenn:** "That's the *start* of the dot."

### P12.2 — The decision (before the decision)
**Art:** Fenn's finger on the map: the **border grove** beyond the crossing — the elven green, and at its edge, a **bleed of gray** (the withering has reached the border). The gray on the map *matches the threads* (the reader's eye connects them: same color, same thinness).
**Fenn (the pilot line, canon, verbatim — the first time he says it out loud):** "I've mapped nine realms for three hundred years. The world's got a *tenth* and it's been *quiet*. That's never a good sign, kid. That's a *hungry* sign."
**Kaelen:** "…A tenth?"
**Fenn (long beat, looking at the map's blank edge):** "A realm. Beyond the map. Beyond the stars. But that's a later problem."
**Fenn (finger hard on the border grove):** "Tonight's problem is that grove. Sylvaris is dying — and your scar just proved you're the only door between the dying and the living."
**Fenn (standing, rolling the map, done talking):** "So. Pack a bag."

### P12.3 — Final panel (bottom strip, dark)
**Art:** The border grove from outside the guard-cord: a black forest at dawn. In the middle, one elven lantern, burning green. And beyond the grove, in the deep dark between the trees — a shape. Not visible. Only suggested by the way the trees *lean away from it*.
**SFX (one long breath, letters stretched across the whole strip):** *hhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhh*
**Caption box (tiny, bottom right, Fenn's handwriting):** *Something in there has been breathing for a year. It just didn't know it could be heard.*

### Production notes (for art & lettering)
- **Color script:** P1–P6 warm amber/iron (safe). P7 white→cold blue (the tear). P8 silver-green bloom (the chapter's only full-color peak). P9–P10 ember-lit (night, hearth). P11–P12 pale dawn→black grove (the horizon darkens as the chapter ends).
- **The white thread** (Kaelen's fraying) first appears in P2.2 — small, findable, missed by everyone in-story. It is the saga's visual clock.
- **The nine-colored scarf** appears P1.2, P9.2 (hidden), and will reappear in Ch. 2 (worn by Lyra) and Ch. 3 (multiplied — one per traveler). It is the franchise's merch motif.
- **The bell** is on-page P2.1, P3.3 (case), P6.2 (the town bell — a different, larger bell; keep the Voss bell *small and specific*), P10 (implied), and will become a carried object from Ch. 3 onward.
- **Milo's accidental Name-Return** (P8.1) is the *first* — by accident, on a human, at no visible cost (canon 03b: "the first person Kaelen saves by name"). **Raelin's** (P8.4) is the first *full* one — the first that *costs* (the white thread, P9.3). The reader learns the rule before the price. Protect both beats: the small one stays small (one frame, no explanation, Kaelen doesn't see it).
- **Raelin** (the elven soldier) is a named Tier-4 character (Thornring's page) — his one line ("the grove is voting") plants the grove-council law and names Lyra before she appears.
- **No music cue on P7.1.** The tear is *silent*. The alarm comes *after*.
