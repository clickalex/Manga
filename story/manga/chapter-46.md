# AURELION · Volume 8 — Chapter 46: "The Wing-Counting"

> **Season 2 · Pages 1–12 · Arc H: "The Gray Wings" — VOLUME 8 OPENS in the realm where the law is not read out loud and not written in the air, because it is *worn*.** Seraphel is the sky realm: cloud-terraces under a dawn-gold that never gets any older, alabaster floors, the cleanest pages in the book — and its angels do not carry statutes in books. They wear them. A halo in this realm is a **visible statute**, legible to anybody who looks at you, and an angel's oath *is* the light over their head: you can read a citizen of Seraphel the way you read a Barrens forearm, and they can read each other faster than that. Wing-count is rank (two, four, six). The realm has no police of its own people, because there is nothing to police: the Light-Orders' law is **internal** — an angel does not enforce the law on the world, an angel *is* the law, and the realm's whole health is kept the way a library keeps an index, which is by **counting itself.** Every year the **Wing-Counting** takes **nine days**: every angel stands in the light, the wings are counted out loud, the marks are cut in pairs into the realm's ledger, and the realm's own rule stands over the stand in plain letters — **when the count is off, something is wrong; and it is *always* wrong when the count is off.** Ch. 46 is the first day and the second day of that count, and the day the number comes up **one short**.
> **Cast:** **The road party** (nine, the box, the cart **Marginalia** — and no hounds, because the hounds stayed where the supper is; **Azrael Venn** — *who is read by every halo in the street before anybody says a word to him, and who walks into the light on his own feet*; **Viv** — *who asks for the statute book and is shown a body, and who finds a draft with a list on it*; **Nereia** — *whose clock is water and whose clock has stopped, in a realm with no tide*; **Kaelen** — *whose boots are up on the box on a rented shelf above the world*; **Bramble** — *the person this realm's form has no line for*; **Fenn** — *whose map gets its first layer with no roads on it*; **Grimm**, **Lyra**, **Morgatha** — *the forge, the grove and the code, all three of them strangers to a realm that keeps its law nowhere but on itself*; **Roderick** — *the party's ledger, and one still panel*; **Sprocket** — *notes, on light*). **Seraphel:** **Archon Seravon** — the apex of the Light-Orders, the kindest man in the sky realm, and the most dangerous («the good man with the wrong answer»); **Purge-Adjudicator Seraphine, the Pure Halo** — his lieutenant, drawn, and *not asked her question yet*; **Wing-Count** — the two-winged elder who has cut every number this realm has kept for sixty years, *and who notices when one does not come out*; **Wing-Count-Young** — his student, whose hand is on the tally knife; **Storm-Captain Gale** — the high-cloud fleet, whose longest line is *held* for the sky-battle; **Astronomer Caelum** and **Fate-Reader Astralith** — drawn once at the star-stand, speaking nothing (their canon lines are held); **Wandering-Order Elder Gravewing** — drawn once at the Edge, silent, mending a line that needs no mending (his line is held); and one **Carved-Order** gargoyle, on a parapet, motionless, unremarked.
