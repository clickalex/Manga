## What each chapter file contains

1. **Header** — cast (with first-appearance flags), locations, the arc beat, the motifs advanced.
2. **Page-by-page panel script** — `P#.#` panels: the **Art** line (what the artist draws, with the art team's key details flagged) and the **dialogue/caption/SFX** lines (what the letterer types).
3. **Production notes** — art & lettering rules: the Unraveling's visual grammar, motif tracking, re-read details, and *what not to do* (protected beats).

## Pilot canon quick-reference (what is already locked on the page)

- **The Unraveling's visual grammar** (Ch. 2, established; Ch. 6 extends): full color → washed → line-art → white (erasure). A withered thing is not dead — it is a drawing someone stopped working on.
- **The scar's cost** (tracked, no exposition): Ch. 1 P2.2 white thread appears → Ch. 2 P7.2–P7.3 threads spread → Ch. 3 P8.3 visible, coat open → Ch. 3 P11.3 longer → Ch. 5 crosses the heart-line → Ch. 6 P10.2 the scar goes *white* once (the only time in the pilot). Rule: *sigils spend*.
- **The Name-Return** (Kaelen's signature Chord, canon 04): say a person's name back, aloud, *with the memory that goes with it*. Names are witnesses, not cures. Die with your name and the gray cannot take you (Thornring, Ch. 5). From Ch. 7: the realm supplies the memory, Kaelen supplies the name — a witness circle, not a solo.
- **The Mosaic Nine** (all named in Vaelis's record, Ch. 6): Kaelen, Lyra, Grimm, Morgatha, Nereia, Azrael, Viv, Bramble, Fenn — plus Roderick (the captain's seat) and Marginalia (the cart's plate). The two empty chairs stay empty until Ch. 6.
- **The First Dawn** (Ch. 6 P5–7): the named Chord of the S1 finale — Everbloom (Lyra) + Hundredfold (Kaelen) — "a grove re-grown, *and* a human's memory of it kept." Its hue is a *new* color (neither Sigil's — "rain on new leaves"; do not reuse it for anything else). The Chord rule (spine #5): two users who have *chosen* each other — Ch. 2 P8.4 (Lyra's new ring, for a stranger) + Ch. 3 P9.3 (the private corner) are the choosing; protect those two panels.
- **Lyra refuses the Crown-Touch** (Ch. 6 P7; canon 03: "refused for most of S2"). The pilot's Re-Oath is carried by the grove's vote + the Chord + the Name-Return, *not* by her crown. The tree will ask again; she keeps not-knowing.
- **Orvane is good** (Ch. 4–5): the reveal is a tragedy, not a twist; the realm defeats him with its *own law* (the grove-council vote, every branch); he tends the garden (the cruel rhyme with the saga's final image, planted). Ch. 3 P7 stays tell-free. His robe loses one layer per name that goes home (Ch. 7 onward); the last layer holds the Third's name (reserved for the S1 finale).
- **Tier-1 pilot lines already spent, verbatim** (do not re-spend; quote back only as rhymes):
  - Arvel (Ch. 5 P2.2): *"The grove's still there, girl. I just… lost the vote. I was never the whole realm. I was one voice, and I forgot."*
  - Thornring (Ch. 5 P6.2): *"The King lost the vote a long time ago. I'm just the last one still standing in the room."* — his Sigil does *not* unravel; Kaelen carries him out.
  - Grief-Old, Mourvel's lore (Ch. 5 P9.2): *"The gray doesn't kill the forest. It un-names it. And a forest with no names is just wood."*
  - Rootdeep's one line, the grove's vote (Ch. 4 P6.3): *"…For."* — the Treant speaks *once* (canon 03b).
  - Faelis (Ch. 6 P4.1): *"The grove votes. The grove is for. So are we."* — the line that starts the S1 Re-Oath.
  - Azrael (Ch. 6 P8.2): *"I broke one oath and they made me saint for it. The trick now is being a person, and I find that harder."*
  - Bramble (Ch. 6 P9.2): *"You're scared of being the prophecy. I get it. I was scared of being any of the faces. Turns out the face was the easy part."*
  - Vaelthorn (Ch. 6 P10.3): *"I dreamed you. I let you go. I am the place where the letting-go still hurts. That's not a monster, kid. That's a scar."*
- **Motifs:** the white thread (the cost) · the nine-colored scarf (the hearth) · the Voss bell (the mortal note) · the empty chairs (the missing) · the count (Morgatha's war-song, the pilot's hidden clock) · the record page (names written down are names kept).
- **Reserved canon** (some slots now spent, some still held): Vaelthorn's S1-finale line — *"You are the only full thing left. Come home to me, little heart."* — was **spent in Ch. 13 P11.1**, verbatim, lettered as an absence in the margin, heard by Kaelen alone; do not re-quote it in S2. The white (the Unraveling's front, Ch. 7–13) kept **no sound, no face, no word** to the end — it left without them; do not retro-add. **The Khazadûm hue** (`#e07a3f`) is the under-roads' answering ember-line only (Ch. 10 P9.2 onward: one line, under the floor, never a bloom, never on the surface); the *stone-song at scale* — the mountain's own consent-light — stays **Vol. 4's**, unspent.
- **No race is the monster** (spine #8): the withered courtiers are people (un-named, not evil); the gray is a hunger (Vaelthorn feeding — canon 04); the Reforgers are a mirror of each realm's worst impulse, defeated by the realm's own law.

## Writing rules (for future chapters)

1. **Canon lock wins.** If a panel conflicts with the cast book (03/03a/03b/03c), the power system (04), or the canon spine (05 §I), the panel changes — not the bible. The quick-reference above is the on-page contract; the bibles are the source.
2. **Pilot lines are verbatim.** Every Tier-1 "pilot line" in 03-characters.md must land in the pilot, word for word (they're the franchise's advertising copy; the TCG and the anime reuse them).
3. **Costs are on the page, not in the dialogue.** The scar's white threads, Lyra's rings, Morgatha's count, Nereia's flask, the cart's plate — the team's resources are *drawn*, so the reader learns the rules by watching (re-reads pay).
4. **The chorus panel** (a ring of one-frame-per-voice, table-centered) is used *only* for the Nine. It is the team's visual identity.
5. **Vaelthorn is negative space.** The art draws *around* him. His eyes are the only ink. His first line is written on the page but *heard by no one*.
6. **Roderick gets exactly one silent panel per chapter** while he keeps the post (Ch. 8 onward). No dialogue; the captain's stillness is the line.
7. **New places named in a chapter get a line in the location docs** ([../../location/](../../location/)); new named people get a line in [../03b-ensemble.md](../03b-ensemble.md) or [../03c-roster.md](../03c-roster.md) before the chapter is called done. (Ch. 8–9's owed names were paid in the finale pass — **Kit**, the crossing child; **Reed**, the court's singer; and **Grain**, the Third named on the page in Ch. 13 — all three now have their lines in [../03b-ensemble.md](../03b-ensemble.md), #281–283. Ch. 10–12 name no one new; Ch. 11's new place, **the Keeping**, has its line in [../../location/nine-realms.md](../../location/nine-realms.md); Ch. 12 draws the bible's sprites, fauns, and mothkin for the first time — species, not names.)
