> **Season 1 · Pages 1–12 · Arc C: "The First Crown" (the call — the realm is summoned by every road; below, the door tightens)**
> **Cast:** **Above (the court)** — Faelis (the call), Thale (the gray hand), Thorn (the gate), Dew, Root, Cael (the grove-singer), Elowen (the bread, the gate-post), Raelin (the first named), **Rootdeep** (the keystone — no line; he spoke once), Vaelis (the record), Reed (the singer — the call, sung), Lyra (one ring more — the top), Fenn (the map — three roads), Viv (the forty pages — the one-line version), Azrael (walks), Marginalia, Sprocket, Orvane (one frame), Veil-Old, Compost-Old, the **sprites** (the forest's immune system — the call's carriers; canon nine-realms), the **fauns** (herd-musicians — the call, answered), the **mothkin** (the realm's historians — the call, written in silk), the wood-elf clans of the near groves. **The Moot** — the ten (Room-Chimera *rooms* the moot; Door-Oni carries the door; the bell on its peg), Moot-Scribe, Wren (walks, drawing), Kit, Bark (the carver — home from the stone), Osric (the ferry). **Ashenmere** — Senna (the sign), Marta (the flour), Milo, Brigid, Ben (the forge fire, in a pot), Warden Gable (the cord, carried), Brother Aldric, Deacon Anselm, Hilda (page 400), Odette (the scarves), Kessa (the paper), Old Tom Rook, Pippin (crosses — not alone). **The bridge** — Roderick (his one quiet panel: the gate, at dusk). **Below** — Kaelen (in the room), Morgatha (the count — stops, and stays stopped), Grimm + Pebble (the forge), Bramble (two doors), Nereia (in the water — the light), Grief-Old (comes down, carrying the kept thing wrapped in Veil-Old's black cloth), **the Third** (the robe begins to un-ink), the white (the ring beyond the water — still; and the stair's first step).
> **Locations / threads:** the great tree + the court (the trunk — the ask arrives in the bark at the hall's height); the FOR road, the HOME road, the town's road (OPEN DOORS — Ashenmere's cord to the border grove); the Moot (walking); the border grove (Thale's ring, Ch. 2 P8.1 — the town's road ends *there*, and the realm's vote reaches it by root); the root-cavern (the water-ring, thinner; the heart-root; the stair).
> **Arc beat:** Dawn to dusk — one day, four roads, one door. Faelis calls the grove the only way a grove can be called: *every branch* — the sprites go out tree to tree (the forest's immune system, doing what an immune system does), the trees lean the call onward, Reed sings it down the FOR road, Vaelis writes it, the ferry carries it. The Moot receives the call at the water-door and does the only thing the Moot's law allows: it votes — and walks. Room-Chimera *rooms* the moot, so the Moot is in session wherever the room is; Door-Oni carries the door, and the Voss bell rides its peg on the door-frame down the HOME road (*if it rings, we write the hour — wherever the room is*). Ashenmere walks its road out plate by plate — the Festival of Open Doors in reverse, the town's doorsteps laid down the white one at a time — Hilda's petition finished at four hundred pages and *walked*; the forge's fire in Ben's iron pot; the cord itself carried by Warden Gable because the margin's job is to be where the line is; Pippin crosses, not alone. The realm arrives: fauns answering Reed's tune with their own, mothkin writing the day in silk, the near clans, the sprites returning with the far groves' lean behind them — and Rootdeep, the keystone, the one tree that *cannot* lean, holding the hall up: his vote is the holding. At the hall's height the tree's one sentence arrives in the bark — and for the first time, everyone hears it; every elf's hand goes to their temple. Viv gives the realm the one-line version of forty pages: *the answer is no; the answer is names; you can give a home, you can't give a choice — you can only make one*. Faelis sets the vote for dusk. Below: the water is at Nereia's waist, then her knees; her light is going; the white does not move — the Third's *robe* begins to un-ink from the hem, the third part of the taking that no one has ever stood in the room for; Grief-Old comes down the stair carrying the Third's kept thing wrapped in black cloth and sets it at his feet; Kaelen tells the Third what his father did inside a door; the stair's first step goes white *behind* the Third — the road up has begun — and Kaelen steps onto it, between the Third and the way up. Morgatha's count stops. It does not resume. Above, at dusk, the Moot comes through the gate (Roderick with it, silent), the town's road reaches the border grove's ring, and the trees begin to lean. The card: **Here come the named.**
> **Motifs advanced:** the call (the sprites — a forest's immune system; a call is what it does), the roads (FOR / HOME / OPEN DOORS — three named, one marked; the call goes *both ways*), the vote (every branch; the keystone's vote is holding), the door (carried; the door's a question — it can be *asked somewhere else*), the bell (rides the door; silent all day; the hour, promised wherever the room is), the plate (the town's doorsteps, laid down a white road — the relay, reversed), the paperwork (page 400 — the human realm's Re-Oath, *walked*), the cord (carried — the margin goes where the line is), the count (stops; stays stopped — the first time), the un-inking (the third part of the taking — voice, face, *the rest*), the kept thing (wrapped; still un-drawn), the scar (the nine colors *rise* from the bottom edge — the tide, coming in), the water (thinner; the second breath — waking escalation #7: a *pace*), the record (the seventh card: "Here come the named."), the white (still: no sound, no face, no word — the stair's first step)
