# AURELION · Volume 1 — Chapter 3: "One Table"

> **Pilot · Pages 1–12 · Season 1, Arc A "The Town" ends → Arc B "The Road" begins**
> **Cast:** Kaelen, Lyra, Fenn, Marginalia, Roderick, Marta, **Grimm Khazdûrin + Pebble** (FIRST APPEARANCE), **Morgatha Ulrik** (FIRST APPEARANCE), **Vivienne "Viv" Nox** (FIRST APPEARANCE), **Nereia Thal** (FIRST APPEARANCE), **Orvane** (FIRST APPEARANCE — a *good* man), **Arvel, the Withered King** (final panel, FIRST LINE)
> **Locations:** The north road, the Seam-cord at the old bridge, the camp
> **Arc beat:** The Mosaic Nine take shape — seven present, two empty chairs. The first full table. Orvane's first sermon (kind, true-sounding, *planted*). Kaelen says the plan out loud: *"I'm going to Sylvaris."* The King speaks — the first line he has spoken in a year.
> **Motifs advanced:** the scarf (worn by all of them), the bell (answered by the sea), the empty chairs (now *named*), the chorus (nine voices, one table).

## Page 1 — *The Road North*

### P1.1 — The road
**Art:** Wide, morning. The party — Kaelen, Lyra, Fenn + Marginalia, Roderick — small, in the middle of the page, on the north road. The horizon: the gray, a thin line where the color *stops*. (The reader has seen the town big; now the world is the size of a road.)
**Fenn (walking, map under his arm, flat):** "Four days to the withered court. Three of them on foot. I'm already tired. This is going to be a good trip."
**Marginalia (tiny italic):** "You have been tired for three hundred years. You are not *getting* tired. You are *continuing*."
**Fenn:** "Same thing, from the inside."

### P1.2 — The scar's cost (the honest beat)
**Art:** Close, quiet: Kaelen's hand on his chest. The green line of his scar is duller now (the borrow spent), and the white threads at his collarbone are still there — the cost, part of him now. He pulls his coat over it.
**Lyra (walking, not looking at him):** "It spent itself. That's how you know it's not magic. Magic doesn't *spend*."
**Fenn (over his shoulder, the number, flat):** "Three names, kid. The soldier. The leaf. The grove. You're running a *deficit*. Learn what it costs before you borrow again."
**Kaelen (one line, first time he owns it):** "Noted."

### P1.3 — Ahead: the old bridge
**Art:** Ahead on the road: the **Seam-cord at the old bridge** — a stone bridge over Veil-water, a half-ruined guard-tower (Roderick's old posting). At the far end of the bridge: *movement*. Figures arriving from the north, walking *toward* the gray's edge — the way hunters do.
**Roderick (stopping, hand on his crossbow, the captain's eyes):** "That's not one party. That's… four."
**Fenn (squinting, the map maker, delighted):** "Oh. That's *fun*."

## Page 3 — *Grimm Khazdûrin and Pebble*

### P3.1 — The bridge-builder
**Art:** **GRIMM KHAZDÛRIN** — dwarf, broad, beard in **twelve braids** (nine tight — family; three loose, unfinished — debt), harness of engineer's tools. He is *mid-argument* with a stuck wheelbarrow. **PEBBLE** (grumpling, fist-sized, a tiny hard face) on his shoulder, *coaching*.
**Grimm (to the wheelbarrow, flat, dwarven):** "Move."
**Pebble (tiny, loud, the whole personality):** "*MOVE!*"
**Grimm (to Kaelen, as if they're old friends — in Khazadûm, everyone is mid-argument):** "You're the seam boy. The one the forest *voted* for."
**Kaelen:** "…You know about that?"
**Grimm:** "I lay the road the news travels on. I'm *very* well-read, kid. It's my *job*."

### P3.2 — The twelve braids (the backstory, one beat)
**Art:** Close: Grimm's beard — the twelve braids, nine tight, three loose. His hands: a dwarf's, stone-scarred. At his hip, the hammer **Askstone** (his grandfather's, made "to ask the mountain things" — the art team's key prop: the hammer is *old* and *polished* the way a family knife is). The beat: as he sets a stone on the bridge, his hand *pauses* on it — listening. (He can't hear the stone-song anymore. The family gift went quiet the day the Reforgers started mining. He is not angry at the mountain. He is grieving a *relative* — the art team gives this one silent beat, no dialogue.)
**Fenn (stepping up, reading the dwarf the way he reads maps):** "Khazdûrin. The name's in the silk. The Forge of Nine Hammers."
**Grimm (a beat, then flat — no self-pity, a blacksmith's accounting):** "My dad's forge's been cold a hundred years. The hammers went quiet the day the Reforgers started mining. So I rebuild bridges. A bridge's a door, and a door's got to hold."
**Grimm (the turn, the forge-knight's line):** "A door that *doesn't* hold is just a hole with opinions. I'm here because the world's got a lot of holes, and I'm the only one who'll argue with them."
**Pebble (tiny, loud, the whole personality, the one idea):** "*MOVE!* …The geode. Somewhere's a geode. It's *mine*. MOVE!"

### P3.3 — Marginalia meets Pebble
**Art:** Marginalia (parked at the bridge) and Pebble (on Grimm's shoulder) staring at each other. Two very old, very small, very loud opinions. Neither blinks.
**Marginalia (tiny italic, calm, professional):** "I have been arguing for three hundred and twelve years. I will not be out-sourced."
**Pebble (tiny, loud):** "*MOVE!*"
**Marginalia (a beat, then the rare concession):** "…You may *observe*."
**Grimm (delighted, to Kaelen):** "They like each other. This is *new*. Don't tell the map."
**Fenn (to the map, flat):** "Too late. I already *am* the map."

## Page 4 — *Morgatha Ulrik*

### P4.1 — The captain
**Art:** **MORGATHA ULRIK** — orc, massive, bone-and-iron regalia, scars written in **oath-script** across both forearms (the art team's key detail: the scars are *legible* — a reader who's paid attention can see they're *words*), one braid over her shoulder that ends *cut* — the oath she's still keeping. She does not smile. She does *debt*. The war-song count in the corner of the frame: **four… three… two… one…** (it never stops, in this chapter.)
**Roderick (stepping up, the captain, flat):** "Ulrik. You're four days early."
**Morgatha (flat, no warmth — respect, not warmth):** "A Reforgers' cell took Tarkhan. And the cells are at the court. I'm not early. I'm on time."
**Fenn (the map, flat):** "Reforgers. The ones the silk calls the *un-namers*."
**Morgatha (to Kaelen, who's been watching — the blood-clan creed, flat, her body a library):** "My body's a library. So far, every page's been a promise. I intend to stay readable."
**Morgatha (next, flat, the Unraveling in the orc's words):** "You want to know what the gray is? It's a blood-debt the world owes itself. And nobody's collecting."

### P4.2 — Tarkhan (the cut braid)
**Art:** Close: the braid, the cut end. Her hand on it — not pulling, not knotting. Just holding. The war-song count in the corner of the frame: **four… three… two… one…** (steady; the reader learns: when it *stops*, that's the panel.)
**Kaelen (quiet, not performing):** "The braid. The one that isn't done."
**Morgatha (a beat — the one time she's not flat):** "Tarkhan. My brother. A Reforgers' cell took him. And his oath went with him — unkept. Unfinished."
**Morgatha (the oath, the debt she's carrying, flat):** "In the Barrens, a captain's word's worth more than a blood-debt. I've been keeping his word for six years. I'm not done keeping it."
**Morgatha (the turn, flat, to Kaelen):** "I hunt the Reforgers. They're in the court. I'm going there."
**Morgatha (next, flat, the hunter's edge, the war-song count still ticking):** "And I'll kill whatever's at the end of the road. I've been breathing slow for six years. I intend to stay *readable*."

### P4.3 — Lyra meets Morgatha (two walls)
**Art:** Lyra and Morgatha facing each other. Two walls meeting: the elf's *duty*, the orc's *code*. Neither smiles. Neither looks away. (The visual rhyme that the team forms by *walls meeting* — the art team keeps the composition identical to every later wall-meeting.)
**Lyra (flat, no wall up — she's met walls before, reading the oath-script the way she reads grove-council stones):** "Your forearms are an oath. And it's still being written."
**Morgatha (no denial):** "Yeah."
**Lyra (the elf's rule, teaching, flat):** "Elves measure an oath in rings. It grows. You measure yours in scars. It *cuts*. There's a difference — but both of you are carrying oaths that are bigger than you."
**Morgatha (a beat — the first time she's not flat):** "…Yeah. There is."
**Morgatha (next, flat, the code, stated, the first time the Barrens' law is on the page):** "A captain's word outranks a blood-debt. That's the code. I've just got a debt that *wants* to be bigger."

## Page 5 — *Vivienne "Viv" Nox*

### P5.1 — The dissident
**Art:** **VIVIENNE "VIV" NOX** — vampire, pale, **candle-gold eyes**, a **Regency pin** in her hair (the art team's tell: it's worn *on purpose* — the find-me pin; if her Council wants to find her, they can). A tailored coat, a **folded umbrella** under one arm, a **sunscreen vial** in her other hand — and it is *sunny*, and she is *out*, in it, in the middle of the day. (The pilot's first visual joke of the Nocturne realm: the dead's society runs on *restraint*; Viv runs on *questions*.) She is *mid-sentence*, quoting the Veil-water to face — the reader joins her argument *in progress*.
**Viv (mid-sentence, flat, the scholar's authority — she fights with a library, and right now the library is *out loud*):** "—and I will *not* be characterizing the Veil-water as *adverse*. It is not adverse. It is *unlicensed*, which is a different offense with a different *remedy*—"
**Kaelen (stepping up, the first time he's argued with someone *older* than she looks and *younger* than she is):** "…Are you talking to the *water*?"
**Viv (a beat, then flat, the team's law):** "I'm talking to the water because the water has a *case* against the town, and I'm the water's *lawyer*."
**Viv (next, flat, the dead's domain — the name as property, the afterlife's law):** "Nocturne's law's simple. When someone dies, their name doesn't go quiet. It becomes *property*. And property has *rights*."
**Viv (next, flat, the small rebellion, the sun — the first lie, named):** "The sun's out, and I'm *out*. My Council says the sun burns. It doesn't burn. That was the first lie they ever told. I'm *slightly late to the sun*. It's a small rebellion. It's *mine*."

### P5.2 — Marginalia meets Viv (two lawyers)
**Art:** Marginalia (the cart) and Viv (the vampire) facing each other. The air between them *crackles* — faint text drifting in it, the silk's law made visible (the art team's one special effect: two precise opinions meeting, and the *wording* of their arguments hangs in the air like sparks).
**Marginalia (tiny italic, professional):** "I have terms of argument. Three hundred and twelve years old. I will not be out-sourced."
**Viv (flat — the first time she's met someone as precise as her, and the first time precision is *fun*):** "I have a *library*. Older than my Council. And it has *subsections*."
**Marginalia (a beat, genuinely impressed):** "…Subsections."
**Viv (a beat, genuinely impressed back, candle-gold eyes wide — the spark):** "…Subsections."
**Fenn (deadpan, to Roderick):** "We have two lawyers now. The map is getting expensive."
**Roderick (flipping the captain's log closed):** "I get to pay the bills. Wonderful."

### P5.3 — Viv's line (the team's law)
**Art:** Viv, at the water's edge, umbrella closed, sunscreen vial in hand — for *one frame* she is not mid-sentence. Her eyes are on the gray at the horizon. Quiet. (The art team: the one panel in the pilot where the vampire is in *full sun* with the umbrella *down* — the small rebellion, made a picture.)
**Viv (quiet, the first time she's not arguing, flat, the creed):** "Someone's got to own the road. And the road's about to stop existing."
**Viv (the turn, flat):** "So I'm going there."
**Viv (the one-liner, the team's law, name as property):** "A name nobody owns is a name nobody can *lose*."

## Page 7 — *Orvane (the good man)*

> **Canon warning for the whole pilot:** Orvane is *good*. He is not a Reforger *in this chapter*, and the reader must not be given a tell about *him*. The unease is planted in the **idea**, not the man. Protect this.

### P7.1 — The sermon
**Art:** **ORVANE** — a human, an **ex-priest** (his old Covenant collar is gone; he wears no orders' marks now — the art team's detail: the *absence* of the collar is the tell that he left, and everyone on the bridge can see he *left something*), traveling as a prophet of "the one grove." Kind face, kind voice. The art team renders his kindness as *warmth* — a faint glow in the air around him, like standing near a stove. There is **no shadow, no chill, no tell** in his panel. He is at the bridge, mid-conversation with the travelers, and he is *right* about the fear. (Canon: the Sylvaris Usurper — the elf's *pride* preached by an *outsider*: "the grove is one; the branches are the wound." The reader is meant to hear the doctrine sound like *comfort*.)
**Orvane (kind, the ex-priest's voice, to the whole bridge):** "You're all going north. To the court. To the gray. And I understand. The gray is scary. It takes names. It erases people. Anyone who stood where you're standing would be afraid."
**Orvane (next, kind, the grove's own worst impulse, worn gently by a human's mouth):** "But you elves know the grove's law. Every tree a voice. Every voice a vote. And a forest is *nine hundred arguments that never end*."
**Orvane (the turn — the line, said gently, *kindly*, and the reader is meant to *agree*):** "What if the wall were a wound? What if the *branches* were the wound?"
**Orvane (next, kind, the question the reader already half-believes, the doctrine's ladder — one grove, then one body, then one everything):** "What if the grove were *one* — and the forest is just a body forgetting how to be a tree? What if the nine realms were nine scars on one body… and the Unraveling weren't a disease?"
**Orvane (next, kind, the sermon's landing, gentle as a hand on a shoulder):** "What if it were the body *healing*?"

### P7.2 — The reaction (the unease in the *idea*, not the man)
**Art:** The travelers listening — and genuinely *inclining* (they're afraid, and he *gets* the fear). Kaelen at the edge: the art team's **one** special effect for the page — for a single frame, the word *"healing"* in the panel's air *flickers* (a half-frame of flicker; on re-read the reader sees it; first read it's a typo-looking artifact). Kaelen's scar goes dull, then bright, then dull (the Mosaic's sense reacting to a *word*, not a person).
**Kaelen (quiet, to himself — confused, not suspicious of the man):** "He's a good man."
**Kaelen (next, the chill, about the *idea*):** "And I don't like the *feeling*."
**Fenn (over his shoulder, flat, the map maker, the first time he says the pilot's theme out loud):** "Wounds don't heal, kid. They *close*. Healing's what happens when they're *whole*. There's a difference."
**Orvane (hearing that, kind — no defense, just agreement, which is *worse* on re-read):** "…That's the truest thing anyone's said all day. Thank you, mapmaker."

### P7.3 — Orvane at the table (the guest)
**Art:** Marta — who followed on the morning boat with three days of road-bread (the town's way: *you don't let them go hungry*) — is setting the long table under the guard-tower. Orvane, offered a chair, takes it *politely*, and the reader should feel *relieved* (another good man at the table — that's the whole point of this page).
**Marta (setting plates, flat, the town's hearth):** "You're eating with us?"
**Orvane (kind):** "If the table's set, the chair's empty. And an empty chair's a question."
**Marta (a beat — the first time she's agreed with a prophet, genuinely):** "…That's the most honest thing a man in a collar's ever said to me."
**Marta (next, flat, the hearth):** "Sit down, Orvane. The bread's hot."

## Page 8 — *The First Full Table*

## Page 10 — *The Night (fire, scarves, the sea's first line)*

### Production notes (for art & lettering)
- **The chorus panel** (P8.2, P9.2, P11.2) is the pilot's signature structure: a ring of one-frame-per-voice, table-centered. Use it *only* for the Nine. It is the team's visual identity. Do not break the format mid-pilot.
- **The two empty chairs** (P8.1, P8.3): plates set, loaves steaming. Marta's work. They stay empty until Azrael and Bramble sit down (Ch. 4–6). Never fill them early.
- **Orvane (P7):** the art must make the reader *trust* him completely — warm light, no shadow, no tell in his panels. The **only** seeded detail is the *word* "healing" flickering in the air for half a frame (P7.2), a re-read detail. Kaelen's scar flickers in response to the *word*, not the man. Protect the man. The reveal (Ch. 4–6 outline) depends on the reader having loved him first.
- **The scar's cost** is tracked on the page: Ch. 1 P2.2 (white thread appears) → Ch. 2 P7.2 (threads spreading) → Ch. 3 P8.3 (visible, coat off) → P11.3 (longer). The reader must *see* the cost growing without a single line of exposition. This is the pilot's honest-magic rule: *sigils spend*.
- **Arvel (P12.2):** line-art court, line-art king, **eyes inked in**. He is lucid, not mad, not evil — *tired*. His first line must be *quiet* (the lettering team: no caps, no SFX, small type, centered). The horror is that the King is *kind* and *gone*. His second line ("I have to go and meet it") is *kinder* than the first — that's the dread. Do not over-design the panel; the ripple-line from his mouth is the only effect.
- **The gray's visual grammar** (established Ch. 2): full color → washed → line-art. In P12.1 the court is *full* line-art; the eyes are the only ink. In P12.3 the eyes are visible *from the tower* — a one-frame detail the re-read reader catches.
- **Pebble's "zzZ"** (P10.1) is his only quiet sound in the entire saga. Give it to him exactly once.
- **The bell ringing on its own** (P10.3) is the first time the bell answers *anything other than a hand*. Keep it rare — the pilot allows it once.
- **Marta** is in this chapter because the town's way is *you don't let them go hungry* (she followed on the morning boat with three days of bread). She stays at the bridge; the party moves on. She is the hearth that the team carries — the scarves are her.
