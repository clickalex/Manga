# AURELION · Volume 7 — Chapter 44: "The Free Line"

The chapter's first movement is the realm tidying up after itself: the carriage on the crates was paid, in cash, at the water-stairs, by the parties, and the realm's attempt to refund its witnesses two chips is refused on the record and entered as a line that moves no money. The second movement is the hounds: the keeper's office opens the pack's **charge-charter** for the first time in eight years and reads it aloud, and finds that the realm has been keeping seven hounds *until called for* — and that the call is the same sentence the office has read out loud at the gate and at the marker every noon and dusk since the twelfth day, fifty-one times by the close of this chapter, unanswered.
