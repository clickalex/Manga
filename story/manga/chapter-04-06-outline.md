## CHAPTER 4 — "The Withered Court"

> **Beat:** Arrival. The court in full. The last guard. The prophet revealed. The grove votes (for the door).
> **New canon on the page:** Thorn (grove-gate), Ithilwen (the ring-omen), Elowen (Crown-bearer salute), **Rootdeep** (the Treant, his ONE line), Faelis (the matriarch), Thale (the deciding vote), Orvane's cell (the Sylvaris Reforgers), the inner gate.
> **Motifs advanced:** the line-art grammar (full city scale), the bell (heard, not rung), the empty chairs (named pins on the map, still empty), the scar's cost (longer white threads).

### P1–2 — The bridge holds
- Grimm's stone bridge across the last Veil-water: it **holds** (his promise, kept — the art team gives it one quiet full panel: the bridge, done *right*, stone "fast when it's right").
- The withered court seen for the first time at full scale: a city in **line-art** (the Unraveling's grammar, full color → washed → line-art, now a whole city in the third stage). The horror is *architectural*: a civilization drawn only in outline, still *inhabited*.
- The withered courtiers: not dead — **un-named**. They move like clocks wound down (the art team: their movements are *slightly off-tempo* — a one-frame delay behind everything else; the reader feels it without being told).

### P3 — The grove-gate (Thorn)
- **THORN** (wood elf, grove-gate) holds the gate against the withered — one of the last still fully named (his name is *still on his lip* when he speaks — the first elf in the saga to address Kaelen as a *person*, not a prophecy): "You walk like the grove already knows you."
- The gate's rule is stated (grove-gate law): *nothing un-named crosses the gate in; nothing named crosses out.* (The door's logic, made law — the team must be *named* to enter. Fenn writes the Nine's names on the gate-stone in chalk. The grove accepts. The art team: the chalk names glow faint green, one by one.)

### P4 — The omen (Ithilwen)
- **ITHILWEN** (ring-counting elder, court-halls) meets the party: she kept the count of Arvel's growth-rings (the rings on his face — elven age, canon). Her one beat, on the page now: "The King's growth-rings stopped a year ago. The grove knew before the court. The court was still *arguing*." (The first "the king stopped growing" omen, canon — the grief of a realm that argued while its king stopped *being*.)

### P5 — Elowen salutes
- **ELOWEN** (King's Guard, Thornring's officer) meets Lyra and **salutes her as Crown-bearer** (canon beat — the first elf to treat Lyra as the realm's heir, in the face of the withered court). Lyra returns the salute one beat *too slowly* (the elf who can't afford to lose anything, meeting the crown she never wanted).
- Raelin (Ch. 1) is *here* — still half-restored from Ch. 2's ring, standing Thornring's old post (his name is back; his *strength* isn't all back — the honest cost: he salutes with the arm that's still gray). The team sees what the Name-Return did to him from the outside. (The reader's rule, visualized: *a name is a witness, not a cure* — canon, from Ch. 2.)

### P6–7 — Rootdeep, the last guard (the ONE devastating scene)
- **ROOTDEEP** (Treant, withering-court — the Withering King's last guard) stands outside the throne-hall: 40 m, slow-thinking, half line-art, one leaf still *green* on his crown (the art team's key detail: the only green thing in the whole court is that one leaf).
- The scene is *nearly silent* (the pilot's quietest page — no SFX, small type). Kaelen kneels before him (the Mosaic's gesture — the reader has seen it three times; it means *I see you*). Rootdeep's eye (a knot of rings, inked in) lowers. One line — his only line in the saga, and it is the grove's vote:
  > **"…For."**
- Then he goes still, and the one green leaf turns line-art (his season ends — he gave his one word). The art team: one page, the leaf fading. *Do not add music. The page is the music.*

### P8–9 — Orvane's cell (the reveal, at the inner gate)
- The team reaches the inner gate of the court — and **Orvane is already inside**, and the withered courtiers *part for him* (they know his face; he is *the* prophet here). The reveal is quiet: he turns, kind as ever, and says, "You followed me. I'm glad. The names need more hands."
- His cell: the Sylvaris Reforgers, in the gate's undercroft. The art team's direction: they are **robed, and their faces are paper-white and featureless** (the Un-namers, shown *as* the un-named — no exceptions). **Morgatha's beat:** she recognizes the *method* (not the faces): "That's how they take a name. First the voice. Then the face. I watched it with Tarkhan." (The war-song count in the corner of the frame *stops* — the first stop — for exactly one panel — then resumes. The reader holds their breath with it.)
- Orvane's confession (his kindness is *real* — this is the tragedy): his robes are heavy, layered — each layer a **name** he carries ("The names the gray 'frees,' I carry them, so the healing is gentle."). The art team: his robes have been *growing heavier* all pilot (re-read detail).
- The team cannot stop him by force (the grove-gate law: the grove does not kill at its gate — Thorn holds the line, and the team *knows* the grove's law outranks the orc's debt, for one page). The resolution is deferred: the grove will *vote* (Ch. 5).
- **Canon lock:** Orvane is the Sylvaris Usurper (03: "the elf's *pride* — 'the grove is one; the branches are the wound' — a human preaching *elf* ideology, the realm's worst impulse *worn by an outsider*"). The pilot shows *why* he was the perfect Usurper: **because he is good.**

### P10–11 — The grove-council votes (for the door)
- The great tree of the court (line-art, but holding Ch. 2's single green ring — the ring has *grown*, faintly, all the way here: the art team shows the ring's edge on the bark, like a seam).
- **FAELIS** (Grove-Matriarch, head of the grove-council) convenes the council: the restored border-grove's elders (Thale, still half — his hand gray, the honest cost; the Fael-line elders) and the under-grove's shadow-elves (Nyxara; **Grief-Old** — who will speak for the King, Ch. 5; **Compost-Old** — the gardener, whose work is Ch. 5's quiet center).
- The question: *may the Mosaic cross into the throne-hall? May a stranger say the King's name?*
- Faelis: "The grove votes." (long beat — the line-art city holds its breath) "…For." (the echo of Thale in Ch. 2 — the grove's voice is *back*; the art team: the green ring on the great tree *pulses once*, in time with the word).
- **Thale casts the deciding vote** (canon: Faelis's elder, the deciding vote) — his gray hand on the council-stone: "I voted for. I still do. My hand's still gray. *Both things are true.*" (The saga's honesty rule, in one line: restoration is not erasure of the cost.)

### P12 — End of chapter (the door)
- The throne-hall door. Beyond it: the sound of a *heartbeat* (the King's — slow, deep, one beat per page; the art team: the page *literally pulses* — a one-mm enlargement of the frame per beat, felt not seen).
- Last panel: the Nine (seven + Roderick + Marginalia's plate on the cart) at the door; **Thornring is inside** (one frame of his back, the captain's posture, holding the hall's other door — "the last one still standing in the room," one page early).
- Fenn's caption (hand, bottom right): *The door's a question. We've been the answer all along.* (Kaelen's philosophy, quoted back by the map — the pilot's running rhyme.)

## CHAPTER 5 — "The Name of the King"

> **Beat:** The throne-room. The King's truth. The great Name-Return. Arvel dies *remembering*. Thornring dies (S1's first death). The funeral. Orvane's reckoning. The door stands open — and the gray is coming.
> **New canon on the page:** Arvel (last lucid line, death), Mourvel's lore (via Grief-Old's eulogy), Thornring (death, sigil intact), Orvane (the vote of every branch; the names returned; the gardener).
> **Motifs advanced:** the scar's cost (the white threads cross the heart-line), the rings (Arvel's last ring; Lyra's new ring), the hearth (the court's table, set), the count (Morgatha's war-song, audible through the silence).

### P1–3 — The throne-room (Arvel)
- **ARVEL**, on the throne, line-art, eyes inked in (the Ch. 3 image, now *close* — the tired king's face, lucid, watching the door). The **Withering** (the Ch. 2 crown-shape) is *his crown* — the reveal: the manifestation was a piece of him. A year ago, the King **chose to be the gray's anchor**: he took the un-naming into himself, believing a king could hold what a realm could not. His choice is *real*, and it was *wrong* — the theme on the page: *even a good choice made for the whole is an erasure* (unity without consent is the same door, painted gold).
- Lyra enters alone (the door held by the Nine; Thornring among them). The rest of the chapter's throne-room page is the two of them; the art team keeps every other frame *off-panel* (the world waits outside this page).
- **Arvel's last lucid line (canon, verbatim, to Lyra):**
  > **"The grove's still there, girl. I just… lost the vote. I was never the whole realm. I was one voice, and I forgot."**
- The white threads show at Lyra's temples (the cost of his name — Ch. 3's rule, paid again, *on purpose this time*; she chooses to pay it: the elf who can't afford to lose anything, learning to *choose* the loss — the arc's spine, canon).

### P4–5 — The Name-Return (the great one)
- Kaelen crosses in (the grove-gate law held — he is *named*, chalk still on the gate-stone; the chalk name *glows* as he steps over the threshold — the grove letting him in, one more time).
- He kneels (the gesture, fourth time — the reader is ready). He says the King's name back — **"Arvel"** — *with the memory that goes with it* (canon: the Name-Return is name + memory).
- The memory (the art team's one flashback page of the pilot — *color*, full, warm, the only full-color memory in the withered court): the young Arvel in the grove, silver-green hair, rings on his face, *laughing* — the realm's favorite, the uncle who let Lyra race him to the great tree and *let her win* (one leaf in her hand when she got there — the leaf he kept for her; the Ch. 2 grove, before the gray).
- The King *hears his own name*. The inked-in eyes **fill with color** (the first full-color face in the line-art court — Ch. 5's money panel).
- **Arvel dies remembering** (canon: "the Withering King dies *remembering* — the one thing Vaelthorn couldn't take"). Quiet. His line-art body, on the throne, **gains color from the rings outward** (the growth-rings — Ithilwen's omen — *restart*: "one more ring" — the art team: his final ring is *green*; the king who stopped growing, finishes his last ring, in the green of the grove he was one voice of). Lyra's hand on his. Her cost: a **new ring at her temple** (Everbloom's price — the Crown-Touch's *seed*: the pilot shows her *paying with a self*, once, partially; the full Crown-Touch — "give up a *self* to Re-Oath a realm" — is **refused** in this chapter: her line to the grove, later, Ch. 6: "I won't give you my self. I'll give you my *rings*. There's a difference. I *fear* the difference. That's the elven part.")

### P6–7 — Thornring's death (S1's first death)
- As the Name-Return is spoken, the Withering (unmoored from the King's anchor *for one heartbeat*) **surges** at the hall's other door — the gray tries to take the Nine through the opening.
- **THORNRING** (Captain of the King's Guard; loyal to Arvel to the end; Raelin's captain from Ch. 1) holds the door. His line (canon, verbatim):
  > **"The King lost the vote a long time ago. I'm just the last one still standing in the room."**
- He holds it until the Nine are through — then the door, and the gray, and the room. He dies **remembering** (his name is on the gate-stone; his oath is in his hand; he dies *as himself*).
- **Kaelen carries him out** (canon beat) — and his Sigil **does not unravel**: the Everbloom ring on his breast-armor stays *intact* (the art team: a small green ring, undissolved, on a gray-and-still body, in Kaelen's arms — **the reader's rule, made visual: *die with your name, and the gray cannot take you*** — the stakes and the promise on the same page; the first time the audience sees what the Unraveling *spares*).
- **The Nine's silence:** the chapter's longest panel — the Nine outside the throne-hall, line-art rain falling, *no one speaks*. The only sound on the page: Morgatha's war-song count, still ticking in the corner of the frame (**four… three… two… one… four… three…**) — the count carries the silence. (The reader learns: in this saga, grief is *counted*.)

### P8 — The funeral (Grief-Old)
- The under-grove's shadow-elves carry the King (the art team: shadow-elves in *light* — their Everbloom-inverted, decay-as-care, canon). **GRIEF-OLD** speaks for the Withered King (canon: the shadow elf speaks for the withered king) — the eulogy delivers the realm's secret, stated for the first time on the page (canon, Mourvel's line, spoken by the elder who tends his keeper's work):
  > **"The gray doesn't kill the forest. It *un-names* it. And a forest with no names is just wood."**
- **COMPOST-OLD** (under-grove gardener) begins his work: the withered, composted, turned into *soil for the re-grown grove* (canon: "the theme, made gardening" — the first image of the pilot's ending-theme: *grief made fertile*, the seed of the saga's final image).
- **VEIL-OLD** weaves the Re-Oath's black banner (the mourning banner — Ch. 6's hall hangs it: mourning and rejoicing on the same hall, the realm's honesty).

### P9–10 — Orvane's reckoning (the realm defeats him with its own law)
- The grove-council votes on the prophet — **the realm defeats the Usurper with its own law** (canon design rule: every cell is defeated by the realm's own mechanism — here, the grove-council vote).
- The vote is not against his kindness — it is against his *arithmetic*. **Faelis to Orvane:** "You preached that the branches are the wound. The grove has voted: the *cutting* is the wound."
- **Every branch votes** (the art team: the line-art trees of the court *vote* — one by one, a faint green flicker up the trunks, the whole city voting against oneness; the inverse of his doctrine, delivered by the grove's own law — the reader should feel the *satisfaction* of the law working as a law).
- The grove does not kill (the grove does not kill — Thorn's law, the gate's law, the realm's law, consistent all pilot). The grove **names him**: his office is removed, his cell disbanded, and the names he carried are **returned** — Kaelen takes the layers from Orvane's robes, one by one, a small quick Name-Return for each (the white threads multiply visibly — the cost, *honest*, on the page; Lyra's hand on his shoulder for the last three, and his cost *stops* — the first time the Nine share the cost by *touch*, the arc of the pilot's team-bond in one gesture).
- The returned names go back to the withered courtiers — and the courtiers' **faces gain ink, one by one** (the Ch. 4 faceless, now *people* again; the re-read payoff, on the page).
- Orvane's end: **the grove makes him tend the withered court's garden** (with Compost-Old — the good man becomes a gardener; the art team holds the image *plain*, no irony in the panel — the cruelty is in the reader's knowledge, not the page). His last line in the pilot (the kindness, still there, in the wrong place):
  > "…A wound you look away from stops being a wound. I looked. That's all I ever wanted to do."
- (The pilot's cruel rhyme, planted: the good man is made a *gardener* in a withered court. The saga's final image — the hollow god as gardener in a dark garden — is the same panel, grown up. The art team may use the same composition, deliberately, in the finale. The reader who remembers will feel it in their chest.)

### P11–12 — End of chapter (the open door)
- The court, half-color now (the green ring on the great tree has grown; the city's line-art holds a new green *seam* along the gate-road — the restoration is a *line*, not a flood; the honest win).
- But the King is gone — his anchor gone — and through the open hall-door, the gray **moves** (first time in the pilot the gray moves *without being pushed*: the page's line-art city, one frame, *shudders*).
- Fenn's caption (hand, bottom right): *A king holds a door. The king is gone. The door is open.*
- Last panel: the gray, at the hall-door, *looking* (the first time the gray has *looked* — the pilot's dread beat, the frame before the Hollow).

## CHAPTER 6 — "The First Crown" (pilot finale)

> **Beat:** The gray comes in. The Re-Oath (the grove-council's full vote; **The First Dawn**). The court re-grows. Azrael and Bramble take the empty chairs. The Nine are named in the record. The Hollow appears — and speaks its first line.
> **New canon on the page:** the Re-Oath (the first realm's), **The First Dawn** (Kaelen + Lyra — canon named Chord: Everbloom + Hundredfold — "a grove re-grown, *and* a human's memory of it kept"), **AZRAEL** (first appearance, pilot line), **BRAMBLE** (first appearance, pilot line), Vaelis (the record's first entry), Baelor (the nine-language herald), Vint (the 300-year vintage), Aethon (the three-day argument, compressed), Vaelindor (the hall of a thousand voices), Bark (the first Re-Oath ring), Root (the first seed), Cael (the Chorus-True fragment — the S2 Thalassa seed), **VAELTHORN** (first appearance — negative space — pilot line).
> **Motifs advanced:** the ring (the great tree's first Re-Oath ring, carved), the record (names written down = names kept — the theme made ink), the empty chairs (filled — the table full), the scarf (on all nine), the count (stopped, this time, *on purpose* — Morgatha counts to *zero*: the first time the count rests, the page says *rest*).

### P1–2 — The gray comes in
- The unmoored gray rushes the court. The art team's **fourth stage** of the Unraveling grammar, first shown: line-art → **white** (the gray can un-name *line-art too* — erasure; the pilot's scarier stage, the white page of Ch. 1's Riftfall, *named* at last: "Riftfall is what the gray does to a *whole sky*. This is what it does to a *whole street*."). The line-art city *crumbles* at the edges (one street per frame, going white; the withered courtiers who *just* got their faces back are inside the rush — the horror of the pilot's middle, paid in one page).
- The Nine in the hall: the plan, in one panel (the team as a *formation*, the pilot's first group-combat frame — Lyra front with the grove's paths opened (her Everbloom, the elf-road through the court, Ch. 3 P11.2), Grimm and Roderick on the gate (the bridge's logic: *hold the door*), Viv quoting the Veil-water's *easement* at the gray (the lawyer at the apocalypse — "This gray is in *violation* of the grove's charter, I am *noting* it for the record—"), Nereia at the water's edge singing the sea's first line *against* the white (the bell in Kaelen's hand rings on its own — the second and last time in the pilot; the art team: the bell's tinkle drawn as a small white note on the white page — the only *sound* in the erasure), Morgatha counting (the count, the page's heartbeat), Fenn mapping the rush (the map maker, *calm* — "It's faster. The map's still honest."), Marginalia pulling the cart *into* the line of defense (the cart's plate on its own axle — the witness *witnessing*).

### P3–4 — The Re-Oath vote (Faelis's full line)
- The great tree. The council. The banner (Veil-Old's black, mourning) and the hall (Vaelindor's, built to hold a *thousand* voices — it holds *the grove*).
- The question is not the door now. The question is the *realm*: *does Sylvaris re-oath? Does the grove choose itself, today, with a stranger's scar in the middle of it?*
- **FAELIS (canon, verbatim — the line that starts the Season-1 Re-Oath):**
  > **"The grove votes. The grove is for. So are we."**
- The art team: the green ring on the great tree *breaks open* — the ring becomes a *line* running up the bark (the first Re-Oath ring, uncarved, grown — **BARK** the grove-carver catches it with his chisel and *carves it* (canon: carves the Re-Oath's first ring into the great tree) — the ring that grows is made *permanent* by a wood elf's hand: the realm's choice, made wood, made law, made ring).
- (Canon note: the Re-Oath is *consent, not force* — spine fact #3. The grove *chooses*. The art team: no compulsion, no glow of command — the trees lean *toward* the tree, the way people lean in to hear a story.)

### P5–7 — The First Dawn (the Chord; the re-growth)
- **The First Dawn** (canon named Chord: Everbloom (elf) + Hundredfold (human) — Kaelen + Lyra — "a grove re-grown, *and* a human's memory of it kept" — the Season 1 finale / the first grove re-oath): Lyra's hand on the great tree (her Everbloom — the rings on her face *turn* — every ring she has ever worn, lit, at once — her three hundred years of patience, *spent on one vote*), Kaelen's hand over hers (his Hundredfold — the human's adaptability, the mortal note in his blood, the Voss bell ringing in his chest — the art team: the bell's note drawn as a thread running from his scar to his hand to the tree).
- **The Chord's color is a new hue** (canon: "the Chord's color is *neither* Sigil's color — it is a *new* hue"): the art team names it — *the color of rain on new leaves* (a green-teal that is neither forest nor sea nor sky — the pilot's only new color; the reader sees it once and never forgets; the TCG's first "combo color," the card game's heart, canon 04).
- The re-growth: the court's color returns **in the shape of a song's phrase** (the art team's key direction: the color wave is *metric* — it moves in beats, the Chord's rhythm, the realm's law *sung true* — the Chorus-True fragment, **Cael** the grove-singer leading the courtiers' voices, Nereia's first line *inside* it (her sea-voice, the fragment the grove learns — the S2 Thalassa seed, planted: the sea's first law, sung by a forest, the two realms' first chord *unsung*, the reader will catch it in Vol. 6)).
- **Lyra's refusal (the arc's spine, on the page):** the great tree asks for the *Crown-Touch* (the art team: the tree's rings reach for her — the crown's shape, in the bark — the realm offering her the full cost: *give up a self*). She **refuses** (canon: the Crown-Touch "refused for most of S2"):
  > **"I won't give you my self. I'll give you my rings. There's a difference. I *fear* the difference. That's the elven part."**
- (The Re-Oath is carried by *the grove's vote + the Chord + the Name-Return* — not by her crown. The realm's choice is the realm's. Her refusal is the pilot's quietest act of courage — the elf who can't afford to lose anything, *choosing not to pay*, and trusting the grove to be enough without her. Canon 03: "Learns that a grove doesn't need her to be forever — it needs her to *choose* it, today." — this is the *choose*, today, the ring instead of the self.)
- **ROOT** plants the first post-withering seed (canon) — in Compost-Old's turned soil (the theme made gardening, *completed*: the withered is soil, the seed is the choice, the grove is the vote). **VINT** opens the 300-year vintage in the cellar (canon: the realm's grief, *cellared* — the cellar panel is one of warm amber and old glass, the only warm page in the white-rush chapter; the century-wine elder toasts *the King's last ring*). **AETHON** (court lawyer) has argued Lyra's right to bear the Crown *for three days* (canon: the "law's patience" beat — compressed here to one frame of him, mid-argument, *still* mid-argument, in the background of the re-growth: the law's patience, the page's joke). **BAELOR** (herald) announces the Re-Oath in *nine languages* (canon — the art team: nine small caption boxes, the nine languages, the first panel in the pilot with *nine* text boxes — the number nine, made lettering). **VAELIS** (court scribe) writes the Re-Oath's first entry (canon) — his page, one panel, the reader *sees* the record:
  > *"Here begin the names."*
  — and beneath it, the Nine's names, in a scribe's careful hand (the theme made ink: **names written down are names kept** — the Mosaic Nine, *named in the record*; the art team: the names are the only full-ink text on the page — the ink that will not fade).

### P8–9 — The empty chairs fill (Azrael and Bramble)
- The sky panel — **the first sky of the pilot** (the pilot has been ground, road, water, tree — the sky is *new*): gray wings, descending, a halo like a **cracked statute** (the art team: the crack runs through the halo *like a river* — the oath broken, visible in the light), dawn-pink at the wing-tips (the light didn't go out; it *chose people over law* — canon look).
- **AZRAEL VENN** (fallen angel, Seraphel, 22 — the team's ace and wound) lands at the edge of the Nine. He has been *watching* (the fugitive, three years, waiting for the angels; the Re-Oath is a *trial* — a realm's trial — and he is the only one who has ever been *on the other side of one*). His pilot line (canon, verbatim):
  > **"I broke one oath and they made me saint for it. The trick now is being a person, and I find that *harder*."**
- **BRAMBLE** (changling, the Seams, ~14 — small, quick, *never the same face twice*, the **mask** — Oni-culture: a face is a door; the mask is the door *labeled*) was **in Marginalia's cart the whole time** (the reveal: the cart's door opens, the small masked figure steps out, dust of the road on the mask):
  - **Marginalia** (tiny italic, the first time in three centuries she is *not* offended): "I have said I am coming, and I do not take my words back. I never said I would come *alone*." (The cart's loneliness, 312 years, its first *passenger who is also a friend* — the beat is one line, played straight.)
  - Bramble's debut beats (canon: feral, affectionate, tells the truth at *bad* times; the team's emotional barometer): she is *already* in the middle of the re-growth, on the great tree's lowest branch, *watching the color wave*, and she says — to Kaelen, without turning around, the first person in the story to see him as a *person*, not a prophecy (canon, verbatim):
  > **"You're scared of being the prophecy. I get it. I was scared of being *any* of the faces. Turns out the face was the easy part."**
- **Azrael and Bramble** (the bond, the core of their duo — the two *empties*): he *stops* when he sees her (the art team: the ace, the calmest person alive, *stops* — one frame, no dialogue, the page holds). ## Production notes (for art & lettering — Ch. 4–6)

- **The four stages of the Unraveling grammar** (Ch. 6 introduces the fourth): full color → washed → line-art → **white (erasure)**. Stage 4 is *scarier* than stage 3 — the page's own margin goes quiet when it appears. Train the lettering team: *white is not empty; white is gone*.
- **The First Dawn's hue** (Ch. 6): one new color, the pilot's only new color — *the color of rain on new leaves* (green-teal, neither forest nor sea). It appears in exactly three places in the pilot: the Chord's wave, the great tree's first Re-Oath ring, and the faint gray wings at the sky's edge (the Wing-Broken seed). No more. Protect it.
- **The King's last ring** (Ch. 5 P4): line-art body, color from the rings outward, the final ring *green*. The panel must be *quiet* (small type, no SFX, no caps). The horror of the pilot is the *gentleness*; the lettering team holds the restraint.
- **Thornring's intact Sigil** (Ch. 5 P6): the small green ring, undissolved, on a gray-and-still body — the reader's rule, made visual. One panel. No annotation. The reader must *get it*; the art team trusts them (the re-read will find it — that's the point).
- **Morgatha's count** (Ch. 4–6): the war-song count in the corner of the frame runs through all three chapters — *four… three… two… one…* — with exactly **two stops** in the pilot: Ch. 4 P8 (one panel, the cell's method) and Ch. 6 (the *rest* — she counts to *zero*, on purpose, the page says *rest*). The count is the pilot's hidden clock. Do not add a third stop.
- **Vaelis's record page** (Ch. 5 P7, Ch. 6 P9): the scribe's hand, "Here begin the names." The Nine's names in full ink — the theme made ink. The page appears exactly three times in the pilot (the entry, the continuation, the card). - **Vaelthorn's negative space** (Ch. 6 P10): the art team draws *around* him. The page's color bends away from the absence. His eyes are the only ink.
