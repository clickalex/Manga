# AURELION · Volume 1 — Chapter 2: "The Name of the Scar"

> **Pilot · Pages 1–12 · Season 1, Arc A: "The Town" (continued)**
> **Cast:** Kaelen, Lyra (FIRST APPEARANCE), Fenn, Marginalia, Roderick, Senna, Raelin, Thale (withered grove-council elder, half-restored), Odette, Kessa (FIRST APPEARANCE), Pippin, the Withering (the gray crown-shape — a manifestation, not yet the King)
> **Locations:** The border grove (the great tree, the grove-council stones), the Crossing, the market, the Voss forge
> **Arc beat:** The grove's gray → Lyra's arrival → the scare → the first *small* Re-Oath (witnessing, not winning) → the team's first half-table → the departure is decided.
> **Motifs advanced:** the scar's name (it gets *called* something), the scarf (worn), the mortal note (the bell answers the grove), the chorus seed.

## Page 1 — *The Grove at Dawn*

### P1.1 — Wide: the border grove
**Art:** The border grove inside the guard-cord, dawn. The visual language of the Unraveling, in **three stages across the page**: near the cord — full color; middle — color *washed out*, leaves half-present; far — **pure line-art silhouettes**, trees drawn only in outline, like sketches not yet inked. The trees aren't dead. They're *un-finished*.
**Caption box:** *The gray doesn't spread like fire. It spreads like forgetting. A withered tree isn't dead — it's a drawing someone stopped working on.*

### P1.2 — The withered elf
**Art:** A wood elf kneeling at a tree, hand flat on the bark, head bowed — peaceful, waiting. On the back of his hand: **gray threads**, fine, like the ones on Raelin. His face is soft. He does not know he's fading. (The horror is quiet.)
**Insert — his hand on the bark:** the bark has no texture left. Smooth. *Unnamed.*
**Fenn (off-panel, low):** "Watch the edges. You can watch a person's name go, if you look close enough."

### P1.3 — The observers
**Art:** Kaelen, Fenn, and Roderick at the cord's edge, looking in. Roderick's crossbow is drawn but *not raised* (he's not hunting; he's standing watch). Kaelen's chest-scar (a dull green line, one white thread at the collarbone) is visible as he leans forward.
**Roderick:** "How far does it go?"
**Fenn (pointing into the line-art distance):** "Past the great tree. Past the council stones. My map says the withered court is four days beyond that. My map's never been *wrong* about the gray."
**Fenn (quieter):** "It's just been *early*."

### P1.4 — Close on the scar (motif: the name)
**Art:** Extreme close: the green line of Kaelen's scar. In the frame's corner, Fenn's rift-glass eye, reflected small in the scar's faint light.
**Fenn:** "You know what your scar does when it's not borrowing? It *listens*."
**Kaelen:** "…It does what?"
**Fenn:** "It listens for names. Yours, mostly. You've just got a lot of company in there, kid. The grove's one of them."

## Page 2 — *The Crossing — Lyra Arrives*

### P2.1 — The ferry
**Art:** Dusk, overcast (canon: Osric "ferries Lyra ashore the night she arrives"). Osric's ferry pulls in. On the deck, alone at the bow: **LYRA SYLVARI** — tall, silver-green hair worn *loose* (against court custom — court elves bind theirs; hers flies free, an exile's tell), a longbow across her back, a court satchel, a thin elven cloak. Under the cloak: a **soft green glow** (the last true Everbloom Sigil — the only *bright* thing on the page). She is exhausted in a way that's *courteous about it* (her shoulders carry the tiredness like a coat).
**Raelin (at the dock, half-conscious, on a litter):** "…Elora's boat. No — *this* boat. Osric's."
**Osric (tossing the line, professional, unimpressed by everything):** "First time a court boat's crossed in my direction."
**Lyra (not looking at him, stepping off before the rope's tied):** "First time a court boat's come *home*."

### P2.2 — The walk to the cord
**Art:** Lyra walks the length of the market street, straight, not stopping. The crowd parts (the same pattern as Kaelen in Ch. 1 — a visual rhyme: the town's two "doorways" walking its same street in opposite seasons). Her mood-markings (elven growth-rings at the temples, silver-green) are *dim* — her own Sigil, low, like a lamp running out.
**Insert — her hand on her bow's string:** the string has a small notch burned into it (she's held a bow long enough to wear a groove — detail for the art team).

### P2.3 — At the cord
**Art:** A Seam-guard (Gable's man) with a spear. Lyra stops. She does not raise her voice; her voice is simply *there*, and the market goes quiet to hear it (elven presence, not power).
**Guard:** "Cord's closed. Crossing's closed. Whatever you are, you stop here."
**Lyra (without turning her head):** "I'm not here for the town. I'm here for the grove."
**Guard:** "And why's that concern you?"
**Lyra (one beat — the first time she turns, and her eyes are old):** "Because I'm the last true Everbloom. And something in this town *just sang to the grove.*"

## Page 3 — *The Meeting*

### P3.1 — Kaelen and Lyra, face to face
**Art:** Kaelen at the cord (he came at the word "grove"). Lyra's gaze drops to his chest — the green line of his scar — and holds. The green glow under her cloak *pulses once*, answering it. (Sigils answer sigils; the reader sees the answer before either character names it.)
**Lyra:** "You. The one who borrowed a grove's voice."
**Kaelen:** "I said a name."
**Lyra:** "You made a forest *vote.* In one town, in one day, you did what our grove-councils have argued for a century."

### P3.2 — Her line (the pilot line, delivered)
**Art:** Close on Lyra — one panel, her face, the old-young eyes, the loose hair, the dim rings. Not kind. Not unkind. *Assessing.*
**Lyra:** "I've waited three hundred years for something worth this."
**Lyra (next line, lower):** "Don't waste it being brave."

### P3.3 — Fenn enters the frame
**Art:** Fenn, having arrived on Marginalia's cart *during the conversation* (the cart is just visible at the edge of the frame, Marginalia's little voice in a tiny italic bubble: "I have been here the entire time. I object to the word 'interrupt' being implied."). Fenn is *studying* Lyra the way he studies maps — with his rift-glass eye.
**Fenn (to Kaelen, flat):** "She's the elf. The last Everbloom. The silk says so — I read Lumen's history before I read my own name."
**Lyra (to Fenn, without looking at him):** "And you're the gnome. The map's got your name on it. I read the silk, too."
**Fenn (genuinely delighted, under his breath):** "…She's *sharp*. I like her."
**Marginalia (tiny italic):** "You like *everyone*. It is why we are always lost."
**Fenn (to Marginalia):** "We are *exactly where the argument needs us*."

### P3.4 — Kaelen's answer
**Art:** Kaelen, finally, to Lyra — not performing, not brave, just *true*:
**Kaelen:** "I don't know what I am."
**Lyra (a beat — she heard that before):** "You're a Mosaic. The worst word in every language."
**Lyra (next line, almost to herself):** "…And the most useful. I don't like it any more than you do."

## Page 4 — *The Argument (Plan / Stakes)*

### P4.1 — Three voices at the cord
**Art:** A triangular composition: Roderick (military, back to the crowd), Fenn (map, standing on the cart's edge), Lyra (duty, facing the grove). Kaelen between them, the fulcrum.
**Roderick:** "The grove's inside Sylvaris. Veil-cord's here for a reason. We don't cross."
**Fenn (tapping the map at his belt):** "The *grove* is on this side of the cord. The *sickness* is on both. Your cord's a line on a map, Captain. The gray doesn't read maps."
**Lyra (flat, to Roderick):** "If the grove is lost, the Veil at this crossing loses its roots. This town is next. I am telling you this as a courtesy. I will not say it twice."
**Insert — Roderick's face:** the old comrade's eyes. He's been waiting five years for someone to tell him the door was leaking.
**Roderick (quiet, to Kaelen, not the others):** "…You call the shot, hammer-hands. I'll stand on the door either way."

### P4.2 — Kaelen's first command
**Art:** Kaelen walking *toward* the cord, hand on the rope. His back to the reader. The town behind him, small, watching.
**Kaelen (one line, to all of them, to no one):** "Take us to the edge."
**SFX:** *(the crowd exhales — a whole town inhaling the wrong way out)*

## Page 5 — *Walking the Grove (Deterioration in Three Stages)*

### P5.1 — Stage 1: full color
**Art:** Inside the cord, first stretch: the grove in full, sad, *real* color. Leaves, moss, light-shafts. Lyra walks point, bow unstrung (she's not hunting the grove; she's walking *home*). Kaelen at her shoulder. Fenn and Marginalia behind (Marginalia, for once, *silent* — the cart is being polite; tiny italic: "I am taking notes. For science."). Roderick brings up the rear, crossbow ready.
**Lyra (walking, not turning):** "The grove-council meets at the great tree. Every tree is a vote. Every stone is a name."
**Kaelen:** "Then the stones are the names. We just have to say them back."
**Lyra (one look — the first crack in the wall):** "…That is the simplest, most *mortal* thing anyone has ever said to me."

### P5.2 — Stage 2: washed
**Art:** Mid-grove: the color drains to a wash. The light-shafts go flat. The leaves are *almost* there. A withered elf (the second one — a different face, a young one, sitting with its back to a tree, humming something) is passed by the group. *No one acknowledges him.* He is already half-not-there, and acknowledging the half-not-there is the grove's way of letting them go. (A rule the reader learns by watching.)
**Insert — Kaelen, mid-step:** he *does* acknowledge the young elf. Nods. Once. The young elf's humming stops. His hand comes up, touches his own chest, where a name would be. He does not turn around. But his shoulders — *straighten*.
**Kaelen (thought, small):** *I can't say it back. Not yet. I'm only one.*

### P5.3 — The bell (motif: the mortal note)
**Art:** Kaelen's hand — he's pulled the small **Voss bell** from his coat (he's been carrying it since the crossing). He doesn't ring it. He *holds* it, and he **hums** — the bell's note, the mortal note his father's bell makes. Low, human, small.
**Insert — one gray leaf, on a branch above him:** for one frame, it turns **green**. One leaf. Then the page moves on, as if nothing happened.
**Fenn (quiet, from behind, has seen it):** "…The map just felt a heartbeat."
**Lyra (stopping, first time she's stopped walking):** "Don't."
**Fenn:** "Don't what?"
**Lyra (facing the deep grove, low, warning):** "Don't *feed* it. Whatever's in there is listening for exactly that. Small, human, *named* sounds. That's what it eats. That's what it *wants*."

## Page 6 — *The Heart of the Grove — the Withering*

### P6.1 — The great tree and the council stones
**Art:** The heart: the **great tree** (enormous, its rings visible as *names* on the bark — the art team renders the rings as faint script, one name per ring, going back beyond the frame). Around its base: the **grove-council stones** — small, smooth, each with a name carved; several are *smooth now* (the names gone; the gray has taken them).
**Lyra (voice breaking, just slightly, once):** "The rings are the names. Every ring is a name the tree gave itself."
**Lyra (looking at the smooth stones, flat):** "These stopped giving."

### P6.2 — THE WITHERING (2/3 page, the scare)
**Art:** In the center of the clearing, between the great tree and the council stones: **a shape**. Not the King yet — a *manifestation*: a suggestion of a crowned figure made of gray, half in shadow, half in the grove's own line-art (it's drawn in the grove's withered style, so it looks like the grove *grew* it). It is *wrong* in the way a face is wrong when one eye doesn't blink. It has noticed Kaelen. It is *pointing at his scar*.
**SFX:** *(a nameless hum — rendered as a low vibration line running under the whole panel, no letters)*
**Lyra (hand on Kaelen's shoulder, the first physical contact between them, low, fast, no fear in it — *command*):** "Don't. Look. At. It."

### P6.3 — The threads pour
**Art:** From the Withering's shape: a pour of **gray threads**, slow, deliberate, aimed at Kaelen's chest. Roderick's crossbow is up. Lyra's bow is *not* up (she's blocking, not shooting — she knows arrows are the wrong answer). The great tree's rings *flicker*.
**Roderick (crossbow aimed, voice flat):** "I have one bolt that's blessed by the temple. I want it on record that I *will* use it."
**Kaelen (hand on Roderick's bow-arm, stopping it — the first time he physically stops the captain):** "No."
**Kaelen (not looking back at the Withering — looking at the *trees*):** "If you shoot it, you kill the grove's vote. And a grove that's been shot *doesn't vote. It stops.*"

## Page 7 — *The Step Forward*

### P7.1 — The withered elder stands
**Art:** From the edge of the clearing: the **withered wood elf** from P1.2 (kneeling at the tree in full color — the reader now sees his face: **THALE**, Faelis's elder, the grove-council's deciding vote) stands. He is half-gray, but *standing*. He walks — slow, deliberate — and places himself between the Withering and the group.
**Thale (voice like dry leaves, but *present*):** "The grove is voting."
**Thale (to the Withering, not looking at it):** "Stand and be counted, or step aside."
**Insert — the Withering:** it *pauses*. (It does not obey. But it *waits* — the grove's law is older than the gray. The gray, for now, respects the *shape* of the law, even as it empties the law's meaning.)

### P7.2 — Kaelen steps forward (of his own will)
**Art:** Kaelen steps *past* Thale, past the cord of the group, into the clearing. The threads pour toward him. Lyra's hand is out, *not* reaching (she's learned, in one chapter, that this is not a rescue).
**Lyra (one word, the first time she uses his name):** "Kaelen—"
**Kaelen (already kneeling — the same kneel as Ch. 1, P8.4, the visual rhyme; he does the opposite of fighting):** *(not to the gray — to the trees, to the stones, to the rings on the bark)*

### P7.3 — The speech (the small Re-Oath, part 1)
**Art:** Kaelen on one knee in the center of the council stones, head up, looking *around* at the trees, not at the Withering. His chest-scar is **bright green** (borrowed, full), and the white threads are *spreading* visibly (the cost, on the page, scary — the reader can see his own mark fraying as he speaks).
**Kaelen:** "I don't know your names. I can't say them back. I'm only one, and you're a forest."
**Kaelen (louder, into the rings):** "But you *were* named. Someone named you. A grove doesn't grow *unnamed*."
**Kaelen (the turn, the turn of the chapter, quiet, almost sorry):** "And a name doesn't die because someone forgets it."
**Kaelen (hand on his own chest, on the scar):** "I'm going to remember for a while. I'm sorry I'm only one."

## Page 8 — *One Ring (the Re-Oath, small and imperfect)*

### P8.1 — FULL PAGE: the ring
**Art:** A single **green ring** spreads outward from the great tree — one ring, one name, one growth. The color returns *inside the ring*: full, deep, real. The withered line-art trees inside the ring gain texture, rings, *names*. Outside the ring: still gray. The page is half color, half line-art, divided by one clean green circle. (The single most beautiful panel of the pilot — spend the ink here.)
**SFX:** *(none — the ring makes no sound; the grove's vote is silent)*

### P8.2 — Thale (half-restored)
**Art:** Thale inside the ring: color in his face, *partway* (his hand is still gray — the cost of the Withering is not undone; it's *answered*). His hand comes up, touches his own chest.
**Thale (whisper, to no one, to the tree):** "…Thale."
**Thale (next line, to Kaelen, the first full sentence he's spoken, steady):** "The grove votes."
**Thale (a beat — the ring is still spreading, the trees are still waking, the vote is *still in* the air):** "…For."
**Kaelen (on his knees, the white threads at his collarbone now a visible line, exhausted, honest):** "You didn't get cured."
**Thale (a dry, almost-afraid smile):** "I got *remembered*. There's a difference. Ask your mapmaker."

### P8.3 — The Withering retreats
**Art:** The Withering's shape *folds* — not destroyed, not defeated. It retreats into the line-art dark beyond the ring, like a tide going out. The gray at the grove's edge is *slightly* smaller. (An honest win: they did not win. They *witnessed*.)
**Fenn (flat, from the cord, has the map open, is *measuring*):** "The gray's edge moved. Four inches. On the map."
**Fenn (to Kaelen, no mockery now — respect):** "Four inches, kid. The Unraveling's been eating for a year. You just made it *give back four inches*."
**Fenn (quieter):** "That's not a cure. That's a *witness*."

### P8.4 — Lyra, one step back
**Art:** Lyra, at the ring's edge, hand on her bow, watching Kaelen. Her dim Sigil-rings — *one new faint ring* has appeared at her temple (a growth-ring, *new*, in the moment of witnessing; the art team's tell: the elf who can't afford to lose anything just *chose* to invest in a stranger).
**Lyra (thought, small, the first time we hear her not-assessing):** *I have outlived three centuries. I have not, until this morning, met someone worth a ring.*

## Page 9 — *The Crossing, Evening — the Scarf*

### P9.1 — The town comes out
**Art:** Dusk. The town has come to the cord (the festival lanterns are out — Open Doors, the town's plates still set). They saw the ring from the walls (the ring's green light, visible a mile off). The mood is not triumph — it's *awe with fear in it* (they're proud and terrified in the same breath, which is the town's honest face).
**Townswoman 1 (whisper, again — the running motif):** "A Mosaic."
**Townswoman 2:** "…That's still not a word we use."
**Townswoman 1:** "Watch us."

### P9.2 — Odette and the scarf (motif: worn)
**Art:** Odette, at the cord, holds out the **nine-colored scarf** (the one she *kept* in Ch. 1) to Lyra. Lyra looks at it like it's a small, aggressive animal.
**Odette:** "Nine colors. It's the most honest thing in this market."
**Lyra (long beat):** "It's too loud."
**Odette (already tying it, not waiting for permission, halfling diplomacy):** "You're wearing it. You're the first outsider who ever wore nine colors on purpose. That's the *point* of it."
**Insert — Lyra, with the scarf on, looking *horrified* and *not taking it off*:** the tsundere, in a scarf.

### P9.3 — Kessa arrives (the news service, first appearance)
**Art:** **KESSA** (tengu, long nose, wing-blades folded, a stack of papers) lands on the cord-post *just* as the town is settling — always right, never on time. She unrolls a hand-printed sheet, sells it, and flies off before anyone can argue the price. The paper is legible to the reader:
**Paper headline:** **"SEAM BOY WAKES A FOREST"**
**Subhead:** *"Elf calls him a Mosaic. Gnome calls him a map. Town calls him dinner (Marta refuses to cook for the press). The gray gives back four inches. The elf wears a scarf she hates."*
**Kessa (flying off, calling back, delighted):** "ONE COIN! IT'S *TRUE*!"
**Fenn (to Roderick, deadpan):** "I have been mapped by a bird."
**Roderick (buying a paper, reading it, *not* denying a word of it):** "…It's accurate. I'm offended."

## Page 10 — *Night — the First Table (half-full)*

### P10.1 — The forge, the table
**Art:** The Voss forge, night, the ember bank lit. A table (Marta's, dragged in) with food: bread, stew, the festival's last loaves. Seated: Kaelen, Lyra (at the edge, one foot out the door, *giving it a try*), Fenn (on the cart's edge, Marginalia parked *inside* the room for the first time, tiny italic: "This is a violation of my terms of argument, but I will permit it."), Roderick, Senna (running the table, refilling, *in charge*). The chair across from Kaelen: **empty** (the reader is taught the table has room; the empty chair is a motif for the missing members).
**Marta (standing, the town's bread in front of everyone, the chapter's hearth line):** "Nine of you, one table. That's not a prophecy. That's a *dinner*."
**Lyra (to the food, suspicious):** "There is no poison."
**Marta:** "There's *onion*. Which is worse."

### P10.2 — The map (the scale, revealed)
**Art:** Fenn unrolls the *full* map on the table (over the stew, Marginalia complaining in tiny italics). The gray stains, on all nine realms, thin and creeping. The reader sees, for the first time, the *whole* crisis: it's not one grove. It's *all of them*, at the edges.
**Fenn (pointing, flat, no drama — he's been doing this for three hundred years and he's *tired*):** "This is what's eating the world. We call it the Unraveling. It starts at the edges. At the Seams. At towns like this one."
**Roderick (hand on the table, the captain, the soldier):** "Can it be stopped?"
**Fenn (one beat — the theme, stated plainly, no flourish):** "No. It can be *answered*. There's a difference. And the difference is *people*."

### P10.3 — Lyra's confession (the turn)
**Art:** Lyra, at the edge of the table, the scarf (nine colors) against her silver-green hair, her hand on her bow. She has not eaten. She is looking at the map's Sylvaris stain (the darkest one — the withered court, four days beyond the grove).
**Lyra (quiet, the first time she's soft):** "My king is in there. In the withered court."
**Lyra (next line, lower):** "He's… not himself anymore. He was my favorite person. Before."
**Lyra (looking up, the old-young eyes, no wall up for once):** "I have to go home."
**Kaelen (one line, the team, forming):** "Then we go *together*. The grove needs you."
**Kaelen (next line, the turn, half-joke, all-true):** "And you need someone who can't do elven patience."
**Lyra (turns away, ears red, the tsundere, explicitly):** "I need *no one*. I'm only here for the grove."
**Marginalia (tiny italic, under the table, to Fenn):** "She will be back in four days. I will write it down. For science."

## Page 11 — *The Decision (the town prepares them)*

### P11.1 — Roderick hands over the journal (the case, opened)
**Art:** The keep, night. Roderick opens the wooden case (Ch. 1, P3.3) — this time he doesn't just *show*; he takes out the full **journal** (the father's, Aldric Voss's — Roderick has kept it since the Riftfall; the small **bell** is already in Kaelen's pocket, which he asked for on the crossing) and a folded Seam-guard order, and hands the journal to Kaelen.
**Roderick:** "The bell rings a *mortal* note. The gray can't un-ring a mortal."
**Roderick (next line, the captain, flat):** "And when you get to the withered court — if there's a door, you don't hold it. You *open* it. That's the Seam-guard way. That's the *family* way."
**Kaelen (taking the journal, the weight of it, one line):** "…You were outside the door."
**Roderick (one beat — the guilt, named, once, in the dark):** "I was outside. I *watched*."
**Roderick (next line, the turn, no self-pity, a blacksmith's accounting):** "So. I'm coming. Someone has to make sure you don't hold the door shut."

### P11.2 — The town packs them (montage, four small frames)
**Art:** Four small frames, the town's generosity (Open Doors, the festival, *in action*):
- (a) **Senna** packs a bag, fast, competent (she's run the festival twice; she's *good* at this).
- (b) **Marta** hands Kaelen a road-bread (the bakery's best, wrapped in the nine-colored thread).
- (c) **Odette** knits — *more* scarves (one per traveler; the motif multiplies). Her hands, fast.
- (d) **Hilda** copies a passage from the journal (the scribe is *always* writing; this time she's writing *for* them).
**Odette (knitting, to no one, the scarf's purpose, stated):** "One for each of you. So when you're lost in nine colors, you can find your way back to a town."

### P11.3 — Senna's line (the margin, early)
**Art:** The forge door, Senna with her bag over her shoulder, Kaelen blocking the frame (the brother, the wall).
**Senna:** "You kept writing 'safe' in the margins of Dad's pages."
**Kaelen (the wall, firm):** "No. You stay. That's the margin's job — to be *where the line is*."
**Senna (one line, using his own philosophy back at him, the turn of the page):** "You said a door's a question, not a wall. That's what you said to Pippin. So the answer's *no*."
**Kaelen (the wall, gone — he can't argue with his own door):** "…Stay in the back. And hold Marginalia's reins if she gets cranky."
**Marginalia (tiny italic, from inside the bag, offended):** "I am not cranky. I am *opinionated*. There is a *difference*, and I will not be discussed."

## Page 12 — *The Departure — End of Chapter*

### P12.1 — Dawn, the Crossing (the send-off)
**Art:** Dawn. The whole town at the cord (the walls, the ferry, the temple hill). The festival lanterns are out (Open Doors — the town's plates, set, for the travelers). The party walks toward the cord: Kaelen, Lyra (scarf, bow, one foot already in the grove), Fenn + Marginalia, Roderick, Senna (in the back, holding Marginalia's reins, *exactly* as told). The town watches. No one cheers (it's not a victory; it's a *sending*).
**Insert — the Voss bell:** Kaelen rings it, once, at the cord (the mortal note, small, human, in a world of Sigils). The Veil-water shimmers. (The town's goodbye, in a note the gray can't un-ring.)

### P12.2 — Pippin (the canary, planted)
**Art:** At the edge of the crowd: **Pippin** (the Openblood, the open-mark on his arm), watching them go. He's never left town. Senna, at the cord, sees him. She turns, calls back — not to Kaelen, to *him*.
**Senna (calling, clear, over the crowd):** "Come when you want to! The margin's *wide*!"
**Insert — Pippin:** his open-mark pulses, once, like a heartbeat. (The Ch. 5 rhyme, paid forward.) He doesn't move. But he *doesn't look away*.

### P12.3 — Kessa's final paper (the gag, the seal)
**Art:** Kessa, on the cord-post, one last paper, sold to Roderick (who is *still* not denying a word of it).
**Paper headline:** **"NINE WALK NORTH"**
**Subhead:** *"(There are eight. There is also a gnome, a cart, and a girl. I have been right before. It counts.)"*
**Kessa (flying off, the running gag, established for the whole saga):** "ONE COIN! IT'S *TRUE*!"

### Production notes (for art & lettering)
- **The three-stage deterioration** (P1.1, P5.1–P5.2) is the saga's visual grammar for the Unraveling: full color → washed → line-art silhouettes. *Every* withered thing in the saga uses this scale. Train the art team on this first.
- **The single green ring** (P8.1) is the pilot's money panel. One ring = one name. It is *imperfect* (outside the ring is still gray). Do not over-glorify it — the honesty is the point.
- **Lyra's new growth-ring** (P8.4) is subtle: *one* new faint ring at her temple. The elf who can't afford to lose anything just invested in a stranger. The reader should catch it on re-read.
- **The empty chair** (P10.1) is a motif: the table has room. The missing members (Azrael, Bramble) will fill it. Keep one chair empty in every "table" shot until Ch. 4–6.
- **Lyra's scarf** is worn from P9.2 onward. It is *on her* for the rest of the pilot. She hates it. She never takes it off.
- **The Withering** (P6.2) is a *manifestation*, not the King. It is drawn in the grove's line-art style (the grove grew it). The real King (Arvel) appears, *lucid-eyed*, only in the pilot's final panel (Ch. 3, P12). Keep the Withering faceless.
- **Thale** is a named Tier-3 character (grove-council elder, Faelis's elder). His half-restoration (hand still gray) is the saga's rule: *names are witnessed, not cured*.
- **Kessa's paper** (P9.3, P12.3) is the franchise's "news service" — a recurring gag *and* a lore delivery device (the reader learns what the town knows through her headlines).
