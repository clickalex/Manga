# AURELION · Volume 8 — Chapter 48: "The Same One"

> **Season 2 · Pages 1–12 · Arc H: "The Gray Wings" — the count's sixth and seventh days, and the first time the realm's arithmetic looks back.** Ch. 46 taught the reader the realm's single instrument — a count that never names anybody — and Ch. 47 turned it on the realm's own, so that the schedule gained a figure and the quarter gained a line. Ch. 48 is the chapter where the realm's **two documents** — a **post** that carries numbers and a **roll** that carries names — are finally made to sit in the same room, and neither of them is the villain. The chapter's movement is quiet and exact: the held place is **held again**; a lieutenant asks her reasonable question *on the wrong stair* and gets a lawful permission that is not a hunt (*the orders will not read a shelf as a court*); on the seventh day a clerk reads the roll **backward** and finds the thing the realm has never once looked for — **the shortfall is the same one, and it has been the same one for nine years** — because a realm that holds a place and reads a number out loud never has to look back. And the chapter's last image is the volume's most dangerous: the count, which has cut nothing but strokes for sixty years, cuts **a word** into the post.
> **Cast:** **The road party** (nine, the box, the cart **Marginalia**; **Viv** — *who reads the note's one soft word and finds the whole of the realm's character inside it*; **Azrael Venn** — *who finds his own sentence on somebody else's line and says one thing about it, flat*; **Kaelen** — *asleep on the step, one throwaway about shelves*; **Lyra** — *one sentence about a realm that keeps a tree instead of a column*; **Morgatha** — *one sentence about audits, said to a wall*; **Grimm** — *the only hands in the chapter that can carry the box of rolls*; **Bramble** — *a line with a place and a date, and a form with nowhere to put it*; **Nereia** — *a loan from a sky that has stopped being generous*; **Fenn** — *a road that belongs to no place*; **Sprocket** — *the notes*; **Roderick** — *one still panel*). **Seraphel:** **Wing-Count** — who refuses the schedule to its face over a kettle, in the realm's first argument about its own arithmetic, and then cuts a *word*; **Wing-Count-Young** — who cuts the second column's second row and the count's first word; **the clerk of the schedule's room** — who asks a courteous question and cannot understand the answer; **Purge-Adjudicator Seraphine** — who takes her question to the right man and watches him answer it *lawfully*; **Archon Seravon** — who reads a shelf as a shelf, refuses to read it as a court, and writes one word in the schedule's margin; **the roll-keeper** — drawn and unnamed, who fetches a bound roll off a shelf the way other trades fetch a tool; **the ledger-woman of the Edge** — asked to count the walking and answering with her book; and a **child** at the wall who asks the only question anybody asks all chapter that the realm answers out loud.

> **Arc beat:** Ch. 48 must do five things. **One — the held place, held again, on the page.** The count's shortfall is not resolved and may not be resolved in this chapter: the schedule's room asks — *courteously, in writing, with a *may* in it* — whether the place can be closed so the columns can be read together, and the realm's oldest clerk refuses to his face, over a kettle, and **nothing happens**: no punishment, no pressure, no second visit. The art team's rule: the realm's first argument about its own arithmetic must be drawn as *a conversation between two men who both keep books*, and the queue that overhears it must go back to its bread. **Two — the arithmetic looks back.** The Archon grants the only permission the chapter needs: the Orders may **read their own roll** (*the orders will not read a shelf as a court*), and on the seventh day a clerk does it — **reads the roll backward**, column by column, year by year, which has never once been done this way. What it finds is the chapter's engine: the roll carries a line whose light is not in the realm, entered **nine years** ago, and the count has been short one every year since and *held the place* every year since, and nobody in the realm ever knew it was **the same one**, because a place held and a total read out loud is a system with no reason to look back. **Three — the same sentence.** The line the clerk finds is the same sentence the roll wrote for Azrael — *wings · rank struck · statute broken · light: not in the realm* — so the realm's arithmetic, looking for a person, finds a **shape**, and fixes on the man in the room. The art team's rule: the word must be cut **small, straight and unhurried**, by the same hand, with the same knife, in the same style as the strokes, and the realm must watch it in silence.

## Page 3 — *The Question and the Permission*
