# 04 — POWER SYSTEM (Sigils, Chords, the Unraveling, the Nine Crowns)

**Design rule: the power system is a contract, not a stat.** Every ability costs something *specific to the identity of the user*. That's what makes it a theme, a combat system, and a card game in the same sentence.

---

## 1. The Sigil — the soul-scar

Every being born in a realm carries a fragment of that realm's Crown Shard as a **Sigil** — a birthmark that is *alive*. It is not a tattoo; it is a *piece of the realm's soul* that chose to be you.

- **Shape & color:** Each realm has a Sigil hue family (see the race table palettes). A high elf's Sigil is silver-green and grows *rings* as the elf ages (the art tells time). An orc's Sigil is rust-red and *scarred* — each oath an orc makes adds a mark. A Mosaic's Sigil is a **Nullsigil** — a scar that *shifts color* depending on which realm it's near.
- **The Sigil is the self.** The Unraveling doesn't kill you — it *unmarks* you. A person whose Sigil is fully unraveled is not dead; they are *un-nameable*. They don't know their own name, their own race, their own heart. That's the horror: the real death in this world is *not being you*.
- **The Sigil is visible.** A character's Sigil state is *always on-screen* — it is the visual language of who they are and what they're costing. A manga panel reads a character's emotional state by looking at their Sigil.

## 2. Resonance — awakening the Sigil

**Resonance** is the act of *using* your Sigil. It is not a spell; it is a *conversation with your own soul*. The user must *choose* what to give up (a memory, a feeling, a future possibility) to power the act. The cost is *always identity-shaped*, never just "mana."

### The five ranks (the power ceiling, per user)

| Rank | Name | What it can do | What it costs | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Spark** | Small, personal effects (mending, a light, a nudge of luck) | A small *feeling* (a moment of joy, a memory of warmth) | The Sigil *flickers* |
| 2 | **Embergift** | Realm-scale effects (a grove re-grown, a tide turned, a contract bound) | A *memory* (a specific, named memory the user *chooses* to give) | The Sigil *blooms* (rings spread) |
| 3 | **Sovereign Sigil** | World-scale effects (a Re-Oath, a Rift sealed, a Realm's heart beat) | A *future possibility* (the user gives up a thing they *could have been*) | The Sigil *crown-scar* (a new mark forms) |
| 4 | **Crown-Touch** (forbidden) | The act of Re-Oathing a Realm (the only rank that can bind a Realm to the Covenant) | A *self-identity* (the user must give up a piece of *who they are* — not a memory, not a future, a *self*) | The Sigil *opens* (the scar becomes a door) |
| 5 | **Nullresonance** (Mosaic-only) | Resonate with *any* Sigil, of any realm | The user's *own* Sigil frays (every borrow costs the Mosaic's own mark) | The Mosaic's scar *bleeds* (the Unraveling draws to them) |

**The ceiling is a character choice, not a stat.** A user can *refuse* to rank up. Lyra refuses Crown-Touch for most of Season 2 because she isn't ready to give up a *self*. That's a story beat, not a power-gate.

## 3. Chords — the synergy mechanic (the card-game heart)

A **Chord** is what happens when two Sigils of *different realms* resonate *together, by choice*. A Chord is not a combination attack — it is a *shared act of two kinds of people choosing each other*. The Chord's power is *greater than the sum* because it is *more than the sum*: it is a *new kind of thing* that neither Sigil could be alone.

**The rule:** a Chord can only be struck by *two users who have chosen each other* (a bond, not a command). You cannot Chord with an enemy. You cannot Chord with someone who hasn't *chosen* you. The mechanic *is* the theme: *unity is a choice, made by two, and it makes a third thing.*

### Named Chords (the "special moves" of the saga)

| Chord | Sigils combined | What it does | Who strikes it | The story beat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The First Dawn** | Everbloom (elf) + Hundredfold (human) | A grove re-grown, *and* a human's memory of it kept | Kaelen + Lyra | The Season 1 finale (the first grove re-oath) |
| **The Oathflame Choir** | Oathflame (demon) + Tidesong (mer) | A contract bound *by a chorus* (a law that must be sung to bind) | Azrael + Nereia | The Thalassa arc (the first thousand-voice oath) |
| **The Scar-Reading** | Titanvein (orc) + Eidolon (wraith) | An oath *read aloud* by the dead (a promise kept in memory) | Morgatha + Viv | The Nocturne arc (the funeral Re-Oath) |
| **The Wing-Broken** | Seraphic dimmed (fallen) + Fray (seam) | A purity *broken for a person* (the light that chose people over law) | Azrael + Bramble | The Seraphel arc (the gray wings in the sky) |
| **The Mountain-Ask** | Everburn (dwarf) + Hundredfold (human) | The mountain *consents* (a stone-song read by a mortal) | Grimm + Kaelen | The Khazadûm arc (the "ask the mountain" scene) |
| **The Name-Return** | Nullsigil (Mosaic) + *any* | A name *returned* to an un-named (the Unraveling undone, for one) | Kaelen + (any) | Kaelen's signature move (the "other people's names" weapon) |
| **The Heart-Given** | Nullsigil (Mosaic) + *a mortal's* | A mortal's heart, freely given, to a god-shaped hole | Kaelen + (his father's bell) | The final act (Vaelthorn tended, not destroyed) |
| **The Chorus-True** | Tidesong (mer) + *a thousand voices* | A law *sung true* by a whole realm (the first Re-Oath that is a thousand voices) | Nereia + (Thalassa) | The Thalassa finale (the chorus Re-Oath) |

**The Chord list is the card game's "combo" list.** Each Chord is a *named combo* in the TCG — two cards, a bond, a result. The game *is* the theme.

## 4. The Unraveling — the cost that is the story

The **Unraveling** is the active threat of the saga. It is not decay — it is *Vaelthorn feeding*. The Hollow Sovereign sips the world's *difference* — every distinct soul is a meal. The Unraveling is what happens when a soul is *sipped*: the Sigil frays, the name fades, the self unravels.

- **Visual language:** The Unraveling is *white-thread cracks* that run across the skin, starting at the Sigil and spreading toward the name. A fully unraveled person is *gray*, *still*, and *un-nameable* — they don't die, they *stop being*.
- **The Mosaic's cost:** Every time Kaelen borrows a Sigil (Nullresonance), his *own* Sigil frays. The more he saves the world, the less *he* is. The saga's central tension: *the hero's power is his own erosion*. The final choice is not "can you stop him" — it's "will you let him have you."
- **The cure is not power.** The Unraveling cannot be *fought* — it can only be *answered*. A name returned (the Name-Return Chord), a memory kept, a grief *kept and named* — those are the cures. The power system's ceiling is *emotional*, not mechanical.

## 5. The Nine Crowns — the artifacts

Each realm's **Crown Shard** is at the heart of the realm. It is not a weapon — it is a *grief-vault*: inside each, the First Dreaming left not power but *the first memory of that race* — the elf's first dawn, the orc's first war-song, the dead's first silence.

- **The Re-Oath ritual:** A Crown cannot be *forced* — it can only be *consented*. The Re-Oath is a ritual where the realm's people *freely choose* to re-bind the Covenant with their own Sigils. The Crown is the *witness*, not the *source*. The power comes from the *consent*, not the artifact.
- **The order matters:** The Seams (the in-between) Re-Oath *first*, and then the other realms Re-Oath *with them*. The outcasts bind the world before the crowned realms may. That's the structural answer to the theme: *the in-between is not the wound, it's the seam where the light enters.*
- **The Ninth Crown:** The Ninth Crown is *not a shard*. The Mosaic is the Ninth Crown — the crown of the Seams, the in-between, the choice itself. Kaelen's "prophecy" is a *misreading*: he is not the world's heart, he is the world's *choice*.

## 6. The Hollow — Vaelthorn's realm

The **Hollow** is Vaelthorn's negative-space realm — the shape of Aurelion *without anything in it*. Inside the Hollow:

- Sigils are erased on contact (a race is remade as an identical gray husk).
- Names are *taken* (the Unraveling starts here).
- The only thing that *cannot* be held is a *name remembered* (Kaelen's weapon: *other people's names*).
- The Hollow is not *evil* — it is *empty*. It does not hate; it *hungers*. The final act is not a battle of power; it is a *giving* (a mortal's heart, freely given, to a god-shaped hole).

## 7. Manga art direction — the visual grammar

The power system is *read by looking*:

| Visual state | Meaning | Panel treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Sigil *flickers* | Spark rank (small act, small cost) | A single flicker-frame |
| Sigil *blooms* (rings spread) | Embergift (realm-scale, a memory given) | A full-page bloom (the named memory is *shown* in the panel) |
| Sigil *crown-scar* (a new mark forms) | Sovereign (world-scale, a future given) | A close-up of the new mark (the *future* is *shown* as a thing lost) |
| Sigil *opens* (the scar becomes a door) | Crown-Touch (a self given) | A full-page *door* (the *self* is *shown* as a thing given) |
| Mosaic's scar *bleeds* | Nullresonance (the Mosaic's own mark fraying) | The scar *bleeds* (the cost is *on the hero's own body*) |
| White-thread cracks spread | The Unraveling (the self unravels) | The cracks *spread* across the panel (the *name* is *shown* fading) |
| A Chord struck | Two Sigils *meet* (a new kind of thing) | A *third* color (the Chord's color is *neither* Sigil's color — it is a *new* hue) |

**The rule: the power is always *shown as a thing given*.** A memory, a future, a self — the panel *shows* the thing that was lost. The reader *feels* the cost because they *see* the cost. That's the manga's visual grammar, and it's the card game's flavor-text grammar, and it's the theme, in the same panel.

## 8. The card-game translation (the system *is* the game)

| Story mechanic | Card-game rule | The theme it encodes |
|---|---|---|
| Sigil = soul-scar | Each card has a *Sigil mark* (its realm, its cost-shape) | "You are a piece of your world" |
| Resonance ranks | A card's *power* is gated by a *cost-shape* (a memory, a future, a self) | "Power costs identity" |
| Chord = two Sigils by choice | A *named combo* requires *two cards from different realms* + a *bond token* | "Unity is a choice, made by two" |
| The Unraveling | A *gray thread* counter that spreads across a player's cards | "The real death is not being you" |
| The Nine Crowns | Nine *artifact* cards (grief-vaults); a *Re-Oath* is a *consent ritual* (a multi-player or multi-turn act) | "A crown is consent, not force" |
| The Mosaic's cost | A *Mosaic card* can copy any Sigil, but *its own* Sigil frays (a *self-erosion* counter) | "The hero's power is his own erosion" |
| A name returned | A *Name-Return* card (undoes the Unraveling, for one) | "The cure is a name, kept" |

**The deck is the theme.** A "Hundredfold" (human) deck is *cheap, combo-heavy, can borrow a keyword* (the any-race deck). A "Seraphic" (angel) deck is *high power, low cost, but cannot be played with "impure" cards* (the purity constraint). A "Titanvein" (orc) deck *spends oaths* (permanent commitments) for power; *break one, and the card leaves your library* (the theme is the mechanic). A "Nullsigil" (Mosaic) deck *copies any keyword, but erodes itself* (the hybrid build). The game *is* the story.
