# 03 — CHARACTERS · CAST BOOK (628 Named Characters)

**Casting logic:** every lead is the emotional "face" of a different realm & race, so any adaptation (manga, anime, movie, game) can put a different lead front-and-center and the world still holds. Every character has: a grief, a weapon, a contradiction, and a line they could say in a pilot.

## The cast book at a glance

| Tier | Where | Count | Depth |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1 — Core** | below (Section I) | 9 | Full profiles: look, personality, grief, weapon/sigil, arc, pilot line |
| **Tier 2 — Principals** | below (Sections II–III) + [03a-principals.md](03a-principals.md) | 29 + 67 = 96 | Full profiles (existing) + function & beat (new) |
| **Tier 3 — Named ensemble** | [03b-ensemble.md](03b-ensemble.md) | 283 | One line each: function + beat |
| **Tier 4 — Named population** | [03c-roster.md](03c-roster.md) | 240 | One line each: name · race · place · role |
| **Total** | | **628** | Named characters in the world |

**Counting rule (so the numbers audit):** group entries with no named individuals (the War-Chief council, the Seam Moot) are *not* counted; the Seven Archdukes are counted *once*, in [03a-principals.md](03a-principals.md) where they're named; the three named dead (Durin Khazdûrin, Tarkhan, War-Chief Ulrik the Unbroken) count in Tier 2.

**Promotion rule:** any Tier-4 row can be promoted to Tier 3 (give it a function + beat) when a story needs it; any Tier-3 can be promoted to Tier 2 (give it a profile). The world grows *from the population up*, not from the cast down.

## I. THE MOSAIC NINE (The protagonists)

### 1. Kaelen Voss — HUMAN · MOSAIC · 16 · Terrania
- **Role:** Protagonist. The "blank canvas" — the only Mosaic of his generation.
- **Look:** Lean, blacksmith's hands, messy dark hair, a **shifting scar** on his chest that changes color near different realms (its color is the on-page indicator of which realm he's touching).
- **Personality:** Quiet, kind, stubbornly practical. Fixes things with a hammer before he argues with them. Hates being called a prophecy.
- **Grief:** His father **Aldric Voss** died in a Riftfall five years ago, still holding the door closed so the town could run. Kaelen grew up believing he was the reason the Rifts happen ("the realms are one dream, and I'm the crack").
- **Weapon/sigil:** **Nullsigil** (Mosaic) — can borrow any realm's resonance; each borrow frays his own mark. His signature Chord: **The Name-Return** — he undoes the Unraveling by *saying a person's name back to them*, out loud, with the memory that goes with it.
- **Arc:** From "I'm the crack" to "I'm the choice." Final act: chooses *person* over *heart*; forges again; becomes the world's most ordinary, most beloved mortal.
- **Pilot line:** "You're not a prophecy, kid. You're a person." *(spoken to himself, to a new Mosaic child, and to the audience — three times in the story.)*

### 2. Lyra Sylvari — HIGH ELF · 17 (elven: ~170 rings) · Sylvaris
- **Role:** The team's edge and conscience. Last true **Everbloom** bearer.
- **Look:** Tall, silver-green hair worn loose against court custom, growth-rings visible at her temples (she's young by elf-count, old by mortal-count — the visual joke of the cast).
- **Personality:** Honorable to a fault, speaks in centuries ("in three days? that's *rude*"), privately terrified of outliving the people she just met. Tsundere to Kaelen: she says "I'm only here for the Crown" for six volumes before she says "I'm only here for the *grove* that you saved."
- **Grief:** Her uncle, King Arvel, was her *favorite person* before the Withering took him. She can't say his name without the white-threads showing.
- **Weapon/sigil:** **Everbloom** (time-life) — growth, patience, rings. She can *slow* a moment, not stop it. Her Crown-Touch (refused for most of S2): give up a *self* to Re-Oath a realm.
- **Arc:** The elf who can't afford to lose anything meets the world that's made of losing. Learns that a grove doesn't need her to be forever — it needs her to *choose* it, today.
- **Pilot line:** "I've waited three hundred years for something worth this. Don't waste it being brave."

### 3. Grimm Khazdûrin — DEEP DWARVE · 19 · Khazadûm
- **Role:** The team's hammer and heart. Forge-knight, heir to a family forge that's been cold for a century.
- **Look:** Stocky, beard in twelve braids (nine are family, three are *debt*), stone-scarred hands, carries a hammer called **Askstone** that his grandfather made "to ask the mountain things."
- **Personality:** Loud, loyal, feeds everyone he meets. Hides that he can't hear the stone-song anymore — his family's gift went quiet the day the Reforgers started mining. He's not angry at the mountain. He's grieving a *relative*.
- **Grief:** His grandfather **Durin Khazdûrin** died in the Century-Root War a hundred years ago — and Grimm's mother, a mortal Seam-woman, outlived everyone, which means Grimm is *the only dwarf alive who can't remember his own grandfather's face*. The mountain's silence is a family archive with the lights off.
- **Weapon/sigil:** **Everburn** (stone-forging). His Chord: **The Mountain-Ask** (with Kaelen) — the stone-song read by a mortal. The scene of Season 2's Khazadûm arc.
- **Arc:** Learns the mountain can be asked by *any* of its family — even a hundred-year-old with a dead ear. The forge goes hot again in his hands, and he braids a tenth braid: *for the family that's coming.*
- **Comedy:** His grummpling sidekick **Pebble** has exactly one idea (a geode, somewhere, is his) and gets them lost constantly. Pebble is the franchise mascot candidate.
- **Pilot line:** "My dad's forge's been cold a hundred years. I'm not leaving until it's warm *again*."

### 4. Morgatha Ulrik — ORC · 20 · Ulgar
- **Role:** The team's blade and moral engine. Exiled blood-clan captain.
- **Look:** Massive, scars written in oath-script across both forearms (she can read her own body like a book), bone-and-iron regalia, one braid she never finished — the oath she's still keeping.
- **Personality:** Honorable, furious, and *afraid of her own rage* — Titanvein means her anger is a fuel line, and she has seen what a full tank does. She counts her breaths in a war-song. When she stops counting, that's the panel the reader watches.
- **Grief:** Her brother **Tarkhan** was killed by a Reforgers cell; her father, **War-Chief Ulrik the Unbroken**, is held **as lawful surety by the house of Tarkhan** — her dead brother's own household (the act-2 hostage twist; the code's *fifth holding*: a debt unrecovered **comes home** to the debtor's nearest blood, and is *held* there until answered). The Barrens' code says a captain's word is worth more than a blood-debt — and Morgatha has to decide which is true when the two are the same man. *(Clarified in the Ch. 22 pass: the older wording “her own son's clan” was garbled — Ulrik is held by his dead son's house, in a house with a fire and a bolt nobody has lifted in a year.)*
- **Weapon/sigil:** **Titanvein** (endurance, rage→focus). Her Chord: **The Scar-Reading** (with Viv) — an oath read aloud by the dead.
- **Arc:** Breaks the code to save her father — and the Barrens break *with* her, the Ultravote passes a new clause: *the oath to keep a life outranks the oath to keep the code.* A new, freer orc culture is born in the shape of her choice.
- **Pilot line:** "My body's a library. So far, every page's been a promise. I intend to stay readable."

### 5. Nereia Thal — MERFOLK · 18 · Thalassa
- **Role:** The team's tide and timer. Tide-diviner who left her sea because "the first song is gone."
- **Look:** Scale-armor, coral-crown, bioluminescent mood-markings (legally legible — the reader learns to read her *before* her dialogue), a water-flask at her hip that is the ticking clock: out of water, she has a few hours.
- **Personality:** Melancholy, precise, funny in a dry way ("I'm not drowning, I'm *on a deadline*"). Speaks in tides — she describes time as water levels.
- **Grief:** The **first song** — the Leviathan's dream-song, the sea realm's first law — stopped reaching the surface a year ago. She was the voice assigned to carry it. The silence is not a loss of sound; it's a loss of *firstness*. She doesn't know what it is to be a world that's heard itself begin.
- **Weapon/sigil:** **Tidesong** (tide-voice). Her Chord: **The Chorus-True** — the first Re-Oath sung by a thousand voices, with her as the *first* voice. The scene of the Thalassa arc.
- **Arc:** Discovers the first song was never a solo. It was a *chorus* — and it stopped because the realm was singing it *alone* (each voice keeping its part secret, afraid of being out of tune). The arc's payoff: a thousand merfolk singing their parts *in public* for the first time. The Leviathan wakes, *hears itself remembered*, and goes back to sleep — gentler.
- **Pilot line:** "My people's law is a song. Right now, the song's out of water. So am I. I think we can help each other."

### 6. Azrael Venn — FALLEN ANGEL · 22 · Seraphel
- **Manga Ch. 26 (P4, P8) — the unwitnessed oath, entered:** quiet for nine chapters, he asks the Barrens' youngest reader the question his whole arc exists to make him ask — *what does your realm do with a hand that breaks one word and keeps every other?* — and then **names his cost aloud, on the record**, because the realm's rule is that **the witness is the one it costs**. Kharza cuts it into the **travel-horn's margin** (her first entry, a rough line, with the cart's chisel): ***a hand that broke one word keeping every other word, and choosing to be read rather than forgiven.*** He is the second entry in the new horn and the first that is **voluntary**; Morgatha's summary is flat — *“it's not forgiveness, and I'd not insult you with it. …You'll sleep, though. The ones in the horn sleep.”* His canon arc (the Seraphel purge, Seravon's *“right by the law”*) is untouched by this; **no glow, halo or wings anywhere** (audit rule, Ch. 1–15 pass).
- **Role:** The team's ace and wound. Strongest fighter; least sure he's allowed to want anything.
- **Look:** Gray wings (the halo is a *cracked statute* — it shows the oath he broke: "I will protect no mortal from the Purge"), dawn-pink in his wing-tips (the light didn't go out; it *chose* people over law).
- **Personality:** Dry, over-precise, deflects with jokes that are technically not jokes. In battle, the calmest person alive — because he has already lost the fight that matters (his own trial, still pending) and everything after is a bonus.
- **Grief:** The **oath**. His original Seraphic oath was to *purge a Seam-town of its in-between children*. He broke it, hid the children, and took the falling. He's been a fugitive for three years *waiting* for the angels to come — and terrified, because he isn't sure he'd make the same choice again, and that uncertainty is the one thing he can't forgive himself.
- **Weapon/sigil:** **Seraphic dimmed** (light that chose people over law). His Chord: **The Wing-Broken** (with Bramble) — the gray wings in the Re-Oath sky.
- **Arc:** Season 2's Seraphel arc detonates: Archon Seravon reveals the purge was *right by the law* — and Azrael's defense isn't "the law was wrong," it's "an oath that demands cruelty is an oath *already broken*, by the one who made it." The Wandering Order testifies. The gray wings fly in the Re-Oath. And the last scene: his trial is *overturned* — and he still walks out, because the pardon feels like the light *apologizing*, and he isn't ready to accept it. (He accepts it in the finale, at the Seam festival, and the panel is him *crying in daylight*. The angels' sky has never seen that.)
- **Pilot line:** "I broke one oath and they made me a saint for it. The trick now is being a person, and I find that *harder*."

### 7. Vivienne "Viv" Nox — VAMPIRE · 15 (looks) / unknown (actually) · Nocturne
- **Role:** The team's scholar and spark. Vampire prodigy who carries an umbrella and sunscreen like armor.
- **Look:** Pale, candle-gold eyes, a Regency-pin in her hair (the "treason" pin — she wears it *on purpose*, so the Council can find her if they want), always slightly late (she's *choosing* to be late to the sun, which is its own small rebellion).
- **Personality:** Sharp, curious, *happier than she's allowed to be* (the dead's society runs on restraint; Viv runs on questions). Fights with a library — she quotes law at you until you're embarrassed to be a villain.
- **Grief:** The **right to love the sun**. The Regency Council (her mother's council) treats her daylight-sympathy as *treason* — not because the sun burns (it doesn't, not anymore; that was the first lie the Council told), but because a vampire who *wants* the sun is a vampire the Council can't *govern*. She's the afterlife's dissident.
- **Weapon/sigil:** **Veilward** (shadow, memory, long night). Her Chord: **The Scar-Reading** (with Morgatha) — the funeral Re-Oath, where the dead grieve every stolen memory *by name*. The scene of the Nocturne arc.
- **Arc:** Her mother, Prime Regent **Morwenna Nox**, made a deal with Vexmourne to protect the living — and the living never knew. Viv has to decide: expose the deal (and free the realm, and ruin her mother) or keep it (and keep the Council, and keep being the dissident). She exposes it — *gently*, at the funeral, with her mother present — and the dead's realm Re-Oaths over a *funeral for the deal itself*. The last scene: Viv and her mother in the sun, together, saying nothing. It's the most vampire thing in the book.
- **Pilot line:** "My kind's been dead a thousand years and still we're *polite*. It's the only horror story I can think of that's funnier than it should be."

### 8. Bramble — CHANGLING · ~14 · The Seams
- **Role:** The team's scout and heart. Child of no realm; the first person in the story to see Kaelen as a person, not a prophecy.
- **Look:** Small, quick, *never the same face twice* (Mimicveil — borrowed shape, found self). By default wears a *mask* (Oni-culture: a face is a door; the mask is the door *labeled*). Carries a tengu news-blade she's not allowed to use yet.
- **Personality:** Feral, affectionate, tells the truth at *bad* times. The team's emotional barometer — when Bramble stops being funny, the reader knows a character is about to be hurt.
- **Grief:** The **name**. The Reforgers took it (Null's "family" took every name, including Bramble's — "Bramble" is a *word they let her keep*). She doesn't remember the original. The whole saga, for her, is the search for a name that *fits* — and the ending's answer: a name isn't found, it's *given back by the people who know you*. The finale: the whole team, nine voices, each saying a *part* of the name. That's the name.
- **Weapon/sigil:** **Mimicveil** (borrowed shape, found self). Her Chord: **The Wing-Broken** (with Azrael).
- **Arc:** From "I'm whatever keeps me safe" to "I'm whatever I *choose*, and the choosing is the point." The last scene: she takes the mask off, *permanently*, and it's the bravest panel in the book.
- **Pilot line:** "You're scared of being the prophecy. I get it. I was scared of being *any* of the faces. Turns out the face was the easy part."

### 9. Old Fenn Thistledown — GNOME · 312 · The Seams
- **Role:** The mentor and the map. 300-year-old rift-tinkerer, last cartographer of the Nine Realms.
- **Look:** Goggle-stained, one eye a *rift-glass* (the Unraveling took the other — he *let* it, to see the edge of it better), rides a cart that is 40% cart, 60% *argument* (it talks back; its name is **Marginalia**).
- **Personality:** Cranky, exact, keeps a hundred maps going at once (the world is *unfinished*, and he is the only person who will admit it). The story's narrator-by-absence: the reader's eye in the cart.
- **Grief:** The **edge**. He's spent a century mapping how the Unraveling eats — and the map's edge is always *him*. The Unraveling takes names; his is the only one it hasn't taken, and he is *tired of being the last one remembered*. The reveal (act 3): his name is the *first* name in Aurelion's last dream — the First Dreaming's last word was *Fenn*, and he's been carrying it since before the realms existed. (The mentor was the world's first *fan*. The map is his love letter.)
- **Weapon/sigil:** **Sparkwright** (micro-mechanics, small transmutations) + Marginalia. His gift is not power — it's *position*: he is always exactly where the map needs him.
- **Arc:** The map resolves. The Ninth Crown is the Mosaic. The stars are unraveling too. Fenn, alone in the cart, opens a new map, and the margin ink says **REALM 10** — and for the first time in a hundred years, the cart *doesn't talk back*. (It's waiting to be read.)
- **Pilot line:** "I've mapped nine realms for three hundred years. The world's got a *tenth* and it's been *quiet*. That's never a good sign, kid. That's a *hungry* sign."

## II. THE SUPPORTING CAST

### Terrania (the human home)
- **Senna Voss** — Kaelen's younger sister, 12. Left "safe" in Act 1; comes *out* to meet him in the Terrania Re-Oath (the act's emotional landing: "he didn't come back to save her — she came out to meet him"). First human-elf exchange scholar in the epilogue. Senna's line: *"You kept writing 'safe' in the margins. I'm the margin."*
- **Empress Aeloria I** — Terrania's empress; pragmatic, exhausted, the best politician in the world and the only one who knows the empire is a *fence*. Her arc: dissolving her own dynasty so the human Re-Oath can be *everyone's*, including the beastkin colonies the empire fenced. The hardest sovereign choice in the book, and she makes it in one scene, quietly, at a desk.
- **Captain Roderick Vane** — Ashenmere's Seam-guard captain; Kaelen's father's old comrade. Carries the guilt of the night Aldric closed the door ("I was *outside* it, Kaelen. I was outside, and I *watched*"). The team's logistics anchor; the one who knows every Seam in the world by its *weather*.
- **Brother Aldric** *(not the father — the father is Aldric Voss, the blacksmith; the priest is Aldric the Younger, a deliberate name-collision that costs him a running gag)* — the Covenant priest of Ashenmere; warm, wrong about the Mosaic at first (calls him an omen), right by the end (calls him a *person*).
- **Aldric Voss** *(the father, dead — the prequel's protagonist)* — the blacksmith who died holding the door; his last forged bell (with a *mortal note* in it) is the object of the final act. Prequel manga: *Ashenmere Blacksmith*.

### The royalty (the nine sovereigns — the political spine)
- **King Arvel (the Withering King)** — Sylvaris; Lyra's uncle; the Season 1 regional villain. Not a monster — a *king who stopped being able to choose*, consumed by his own realm's rot. His last lucid line (to Lyra): *"The grove's still there, girl. I just... lost the vote. I was never the whole realm. I was one voice, and I forgot."* The Withering King dies *remembering* — the one thing Vaelthorn couldn't take.
- **Grand-Anvil Khazur** — Khazadûm's king; warm, gruff, the mountain's *lawyer* (he speaks for the mountain in the Ultravotes). His arc: choosing to *fight open* instead of sealing the mountain — and the mountain, asked by a dwarf who *can* hear it again, consents.
- **Thalarch Maelis** — Thalassa's matriarch; Nereia's mentor; the last voice that *remembered* the first song as a solo (her grief: she sang it alone for a century, and that's why it went quiet — she was *afraid of the chorus*).
- **The Seven Archdukes** — Emberfall's Fire-Court; a *committee* of oaths. Their arc: re-contracting Malachar (the public trial of oaths where every demon testifies). The Archdukes are the most *fun* villains-to-allies conversion in the book.
- **Archon Seravon** — Seraphel; the "good man with the wrong answer." The Purity arc's engine. Not evil — *genuinely forgotten why the light protects anyone*. His final line (after the gray wings fly): *"I kept the law. You kept the people. I don't know which of us is the wren and which is the sky."* (He never resolves it. That's the point — some oaths don't get pardons, they get *companions*.)
- **Prime Regent Morwenna Nox** — Nocturne's vampire regent; Viv's mother; made the deal with Vexmourne to protect the living. The dead's realm's hardest choice, and she makes it in the same scene as Aeloria — *the two hardest choices in the book happen on the same page, in different realms, at the same moment*. (The panel layout: split down the middle, one human desk, one vampire desk, same gesture, different light.)
- **The War-Chief council (Ulgar)** — the Ultravote; the Barrens' "king" is a *procedure*, not a person. The council's arc is *Morgatha's* arc — the code rewrites itself around her choice.
- **The Seam Moot** — the Seams' "sovereign": no crown, no throne, a *room* (the Moot is a *room*, and the room is the law). The Moot's one rule: *a name is a home*.

### The other nine (recurring, per-realm color)
- **Kiln** — a golem (Khazadûm); the "First Waking" — a servant-golem who *asked* to be a person, and the mountain said *yes* (the first *consent* of stone in the book). Season 2's Khazadûm heart.
- **Pebble** — a grummpling; Grimm's sidekick; one idea (a geode, somewhere, is his); the franchise mascot candidate.
- **The Siren judge, Vox-Cantor Iliana** — Thalassa's law-court singer; her verdicts are duets; the most *famous* duet in the sea is the one she sang *to the Leviathan*, the night the first song went quiet (she was the only one who heard it stop — and she *kept singing*, alone, for a century, so the realm wouldn't notice. The chorus arc's *real* tragedy: the realm's law was carried by *one voice*, and the voice was *dying*).
- **The Tengu news-seller, Wingblade Kessa** — the franchise's "news service"; sells truth by the blade's length; the running-gag vendor who is *always* right and *never* on time.
- **The Oni mask-keeper, Tessen-Old** — the Seams' protectors; the "I am what I put on, and I take it off" philosophy, *made flesh*; Bramble's father-figure (he *chose* to be a father to a changeling, and the mask he gave her is the *labeled* door).
- **The Revenant captain, Oathgrave Yael** — the Nightwatch's dead-knight; guards the Veil from the other side; the quiet recurring guard-force; the *one* character who is *already* in the Unraveling (her name is fading, and she *knows*, and she keeps the watch anyway — the dead's answer to the Unraveling: *a name kept by the dead is a name that can't be taken*).

## III. THE VILLAINS

### Vaelthorn, the Hollow Sovereign — THE MAIN ANTAGONIST
- **What he is:** Aurelion's negative image — the shape of the First Dreaming *without anything in it*. Not a fallen god; the *regret* of a choice (the moment between "dreaming everything" and "letting it go," where the dreamer still wanted to keep the dreamers).
- **What he wants:** To be *filled* — by consuming all distinct life, remelting every race into one painless unity (the Reforging). His wholeness would be a *hollow* wholeness: one being, no others, no choice.
- **Why he's right (the trap):** The world *is* in pain. Unity *would* end pain. He is not wrong about the diagnosis — only about the cure. The hero must answer him with a *better wholeness*, not more force.
- **His voice:** Gentle, patient, *tired*. He has been empty for a thousand years, and the emptiness is *boring*. He speaks like a parent who has given up on the child — which is the most dangerous kind of love there is.
- **His line (the first, Season 1 finale):** *"You are the only full thing left. Come home to me, little heart."*
- **His ending (the final act):** Not destroyed — *tended*. Kaelen gives him the one thing a hollow can hold: a mortal's heart (Aldric's bell), freely given. The Hollow collapses into a dark garden under the world, where the Unraveling grows back as *soil* — grief made fertile. The final image of the villain: not ash, but a *gardener*, in a dark garden, *planting*.
- **Pilot (prequel) line:** *"I dreamed you. I let you go. I am the place where the letting-go still hurts. That's not a monster, kid. That's a* scar."*

### The Council of Embers — THE THREE USURPERS (Season 2's three big villains)
Each is a broken genius from a different realm, serving the Reforging for a *different* (and wrong) reason. The season's rhythm: one Usurper, one realm, one law.

1. **Malachar, the Oath-Eater** — DEMON (Emberfall). An Archduke who broke his *own* Oathfire and *survived* (he found a clause — the djinn Scribe's "perfectly drafted" clause — that made his oath *void*, and he made a religion of *void oaths*). He breaks contracts for sport and has made a *theology* of it ("a promise is a cage; I am the key"). His defeated: the Fire-Court *re-contracts* him — every demon in the realm testifies to what the oaths were worth to them, and Malachar, *against his will*, is made to *keep* every promise he makes forever (his Oathfire, inverted, becomes a chain). The final image: Malachar, *crying*, as he *keeps* a promise he never wanted to make — and the promise is *small* (to water a salamander's hot-spring, every dawn). The villain defeated by his own people's law, and the law is *gentle*.
   - **Line:** *"I broke the first oath. I am the* door. *Everything after me is* free."

2. **Vexmourne the Lich** — UNDEAD RANK (Nocturne). A wraith who *refused* to move on by *choice* (the "Longgrace") — and turned his refusal into a *bank*: he's been *banking* other people's afterlives (their memories, their names, their second lives) for a thousand years. The Nocturne arc's engine: the dead's realm is being *hollowed from the inside* because Vexmourne is *holding* their memories hostage ("I'm not *stealing* them. I'm *keeping* them. You were going to *lose* them anyway — I'm just* keeping *them, so you can't* forget *me"). Viv's mother's deal with him is the arc's knife. His defeated: the funeral Re-Oath — the dead grieve every stolen memory *by name*, and the grief itself becomes the Sigil that re-binds the realm (a name *spoken in grief* is a name Vexmourne *can't bank*, because the grief is *theirs*, and he has no *grief* to bank — he is a Lich, and a Lich *cannot grieve*, and that is the *hole* in his vault).
   - **Line:** *"You call it theft. I call it* preservation. *You're going to* lose *them, Viv. I'm just the only one who* kept *them. That's not a* crime. *That's a* mercy."

3. **Gorakh the Scarred** — CYCLOPE (Ulgar). **Ch. 28, on the page at last, drawn small:** the founder of the first cell comes back off the grey at the head of four samplers, in a plain grey coat with **a mender's cloth sewn over the pocket** and a long-handled scoop over his shoulder; his scoops live on a rack, eleven of them worn out and none thrown away; he pours tea without being asked; his reaction to the realm's complaint is **relief**. He **concedes the case and overpays it** (the third crossing's fee doubled, out of the survey's own purse — a month of his samplers' wages), signs the letter reading *this camp was wrong at the third crossing, and it was wrong lawfully, and the two are the same problem*, and tells Tarkhan's sister the truth: not *I killed him* but ***I used him*** — a dead man's debt as a lever, because the Barrens had the only procedure in nine realms that could invent a law in a week and the wound does not wait a century. **He does not apologise for the lever**; he apologises for the year, and he would do it again. His answer to *why* is the character: *you're the first realm that's ever come here with a reader instead of an army.* His only unguarded speech is about the **fourth cell** (*they read — they use the same rules the realm does*), and his teacher's eight words in a camp yard are the rest of his life. A forge-sage whose family was lost to a *Rift* (the Veil's tearing, the Unraveling's first visible wound). He blames the *realms* — not the Reforgers, the *realms themselves* ("the Veil is a *scar*; the realms are the *wound*; the Reforging is the* only *stitch"). He is the Usurper the reader *almost* agrees with — his grief is *legible*, his rage is *earned*, and his plan is *almost* right (the realms *are* the wound; the Reforging *is* a stitch). The Barrens' answer to him is *Morgatha's* answer: the code rewrites itself around the choice to *keep a life* — and the Ultravote passes, and Gorakh, *alone*, is the only voice that says *no* — and the Barrens, *with one throat*, say *"we're sorry, Gorakh. The code's* changed. *You can* stay. *You can't* stitch." His defeated: he *walks out* of the Ultravote, *alone*, into the Rift he's been blaming — and the last image is him *on the other side*, *looking back*, and the Rift *closes behind him* (he is not *killed*; he is *left* — the Barrens' way of saying *we tried*).
   - **Line:** *"The Veil's a* scar. *The realms are the* wound. *The Reforging's the only* stitch. *I'm not the* villain. *I'm the* doctor."

### The Reforgers — THE CULT (recurring, cell-based)
A cross-realm cult of the desperate and the ambitious. Preach: the realms must be "reforged" — reforged into one. Each realm arc has a *cell*, led by a named leader who is a *mirror* of the realm's own worst impulse:

| Cell (realm) | Leader | The mirror (the realm's worst impulse, made flesh) |
|---|---|---|
| **Sylvaris** (S1) | **Prophet Orvane** (a human, ex-priest) | The elf's *pride* — "the grove is *one*; the *branches* are the wound" (a human preaching *elf* ideology, the realm's worst impulse *worn by an outsider*) |
| **Khazadûm** (S2) | **The Miner, Stonehand Drek** (a flame dwarf; **Vol. 4 beat, Ch. 14–15:** his cell *buys* rather than breaks — a well-run mine whose contracts are **sworn *by* the mountain** (*the theft of a witness*), and who moves the realm’s court of account into his own galleries by law, not force) | The dwarf's *grief* — "the mountain's a *relative*; the *mining* is the *funeral*; the Reforging is the *last* braid" (a dwarf who *can't hear the stone-song*, and has made a *theology* of the silence; **Vol. 4 note: he is warm, he is a mason, and he keeps every promise he makes** — his braids are shorn as *mourning dress*, “a braid’s a promise to keep listening” (Ch. 14–15); dangerous precisely because he is a man of his word) |
| **Ulgar** (S2) | **Gorakh the Scarred** (a cyclops) | The orc's *code* — "the *honor* is the *wound*; the *Reforging* is the *one* throat" (the code, *taken to its logical end*) |
| **Thalassa** (S2) | **The Silencer, Vox-Nul** (a siren) | The merfolk's *law* — "the *chorus* is the *wound*; the *silence* is the *one* voice" (the law, *sung* into a *void*) |
| **Emberfall** (S2) | **Malachar, the Oath-Eater** (a demon) | The demon's *oath* — "the *contract* is the *cage*; the *Reforging* is the *key*" (the law, *broken* into a *religion*) |
| **Seraphel** (S2) | **The Purifier, Halo-Null** (a fallen angel) | The angel's *purity* — "the *light* is the *wound*; the *Reforging* is the *one* wing" (the order, *dimmed* into a *void*) |
| **Nocturne** (S2) | **Vexmourne the Lich** (a lich) | The dead's *memory* — "the *name* is the *wound*; the *Reforging* is the *one* silence" (the afterlife, *banked* into a *vault*) |
| **Terrania** (S2) | **The Fence, Irongate Vane** (a human, Roderick's brother) | The human's *institution* — "the *law* is the *wound*; the *Reforging* is the *one* court" (the hundred courts, *closed* into a *single* desk) |
| **The Seams** (S2) | **Null** (a Mosaic) | The Mosaic's *choice* — "the *self* is the *wound*; the *Reforging* is the *one* face" (the in-between, *erased* into a *hollow*) |

**The Reforgers' design rule:** every cell leader is a *mirror* of the realm's own worst impulse — and the realm defeats them *with its own law* (the grove-council, the stone-song, the Ultravote, the chorus-trial, the Fire-Court, the purity-council, the name-funeral, the popular oath, the seam-moot). The team wins; the realm does the healing. That's the formula.

### Null — THE DARK TWIN (Kaelen's mirror)
- **What she is:** A Mosaic like Kaelen, raised by the Reforgers in Nocturne, already *given herself to the Hollow*. Vaelthorn's weapon, and Kaelen's dark twin.
- **What she has that Kaelen wants:** No pain, no doubt, a family of the Reforgers who "belong."
- **What Kaelen has that she'd trade for it:** A name (the Reforgers took hers — "Null" is a *word they let her keep*, the same way "Bramble" is a word they let *her* keep — the two are *sisters in the taking*, and the finale's mirror is *the two of them, choosing different answers to the same question*).
- **Her defeated:** Kaelen doesn't beat her by being stronger. He beats her by *refusing to be the thing she hates*: "You're not me. You're what I'd be if I stopped being mine." He breaks her hollow by handing her back her own name — and the moment Null remembers her name, the Hollow *cannot hold her*. (Names are the most important thing in this world. The Unraveling starts by taking them. Kaelen's whole weapon is other people's names. And the last name he returns is *her own*.)
- **Line (the mirror, the finale):** *"I was* you. *I just* stopped. *You're going to* stop. *That's not a* threat. *That's a* schedule."
