# 03c — TIER 4 · NAMED POPULATION ROSTER (240)

**What this file is:** the fourth and densest ring of *named* characters — the **named population** of each realm. These are the *background* people: family of the ensemble, workers, students, soldiers, travelers, and named NPCs who make each realm feel *lived-in*. Each entry is one line: **Name · Race · Place · Role.**

**Count:** 240 named population characters (24 per realm × 9 realms + 25 cross-realm).

**How to use:** this is the **world-density layer** and the **spin-off seed bank**. Any row can be promoted:
- To a **Tier-3 ensemble** member (earn a function + beat) when a story needs them.
- To a **TCG/PC-game NPC** (quest-giver, shopkeeper, deck-flavor).
- To a **spin-off lead** (the "world is the IP; the characters are the doors" rule).

**Naming logic:** half the entries are **kin of the named ensemble** (tying the population to the cast — the world is one connected place); the other half are **distinct-role** named people. No name collides with Tiers 1–3.
