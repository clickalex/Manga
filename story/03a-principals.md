# 03a — TIER 2 · NAMED PRINCIPALS (67 New)

**What this file is:** the next ring of *named* characters beyond the 9 leads and the 30 already-profiled principals/villains in [03-characters.md](03-characters.md). These are the **sovereigns, courtiers, clan-leaders, temple-keepers, family, and recurring realm-color** — the people the team meets *in each realm*, and the people who *become* the realm's answer.

**Count:** 67 new named principals here + 29 existing (in 03-characters.md) = **96 Tier-2 principals.** Every one has a *function* (a role in the realm) and a *beat* (a story function). None is a monster — each is a person with a job, a grievance, and a choice.

**Naming note:** surnames echo the existing cast families (Voss, Vane, Nox, Ulrik, Thal, Venn, Khazdûrin, Thistledown, Sylvari) where kinship is intended; everything else is new. No name collides with the Tier-1 or existing Tier-2.

## Terrania (+8)

### Chancellor Petra Aldous — HUMAN · the Concord court
- **Function:** the Empress's chancellor; architect of the **Fence Laws** (the empire's walling of the beastkin colonies).
- **Beat:** Aeloria's *mirror* — she believes fences protect. The Terrania arc's knife: it's the fence's *architect* who must *open* the fence (she dismantles the Laws in one scene, quietly, at a desk — the parallel to Aeloria's dissolution, two pages apart).
- **Line:** *"I built the fence to keep you safe. I'm opening it because you were never the threat. The fence was."*

### General Corin Halloway — HUMAN · the Concord war-ministry
- **Function:** commander of the fence-line; Aeloria's war-minister; professional, blunt, the one who tells the Empress the truth when it's uncomfortable.
- **Beat:** the army's face in the Re-Oath. He *disbands* the fence-line in the Re-Oath scene (the "soldier's doubt" made flesh — a man who's been guarding a wall for thirty years, choosing to stand down).
- **Line:** *"I've spent thirty years guarding a wall. Today I learned the wall was the thing I was supposed to guard *against*."*

### Guildmaster Josiah Crane — HUMAN · the Hundred Courts
- **Function:** Chief Arbiter of the Hundred Courts; the most famous lawyer in Terrania; argues for *fun*.
- **Beat:** runs Kaelen's **citizenship case** (the Mosaic's first public legal moment — is a Mosaic a person, a prophecy, or a *national security risk*? Crane argues *person*, and wins, and the verdict is the saga's first "the law chose a person" beat).
- **Line:** *"The Court can rule he's an omen. I intend to make it rule he's a* client."

### Marta Vell — HUMAN · Ashenmere
- **Function:** the town baker; Senna's first friend's mother; the hearth's extension.
- **Beat:** her kitchen is where the Mosaic Nine share their *first meal* together (the "Hearthbound" moment — the town's warmth made flesh; the first time the team is a *table* instead of a group).
- **Line:** *"Nine of you, one table. That's not a prophecy. That's a* dinner."

### Tessa the Bearline — BEASTKIN (bearline) · the Fenceline
- **Function:** matriarch of the Fenceline colony; the Fence arc's *opposing* voice; not angry — *tired*.
- **Beat:** ends the Fence arc with the question that breaks Aeloria's fence: *"If you can't even give us a name on the map, how can you call it protection?"* (The empire's maps literally *don't name* the Fenceline — the prejudice made cartographic.)
- **Line:** *"You drew a line and called it a border. We drew a home and called it a name. Guess which one's going to outlast the ink."*

### Corvin Aldous — OPENBLOOD (human-kin) · the Concord
- **Function:** Petra's son; Kaelen's peer; an Openblood (visible borrowed Sigil) and a *genius* — kept out of the court for his mark.
- **Beat:** the prejudice canary. His Re-Oath moment: he signs *first*, in public, Sigil burning — the canary becomes the first bird through the opened cage.
- **Line:** *"My mark's not a stain. It's a* signature. *Someone in this room taught me that, and I'm signing with it."*

### Wren Thistledown — GNOME · the Seams
- **Function:** Fenn's niece, 40; apprentice cartographer; inherits the Marginalia's seat in the epilogue.
- **Beat:** the franchise's "cartographer's successor" — the sequel's seed on the human scale (the world's map passes from the old gnome's hand to the young one's; the "REALM 10" ink is hers to read next).
- **Line:** *"Uncle Fenn kept the map honest. I'm going to keep it* open."

### Dockmaster "Hal" Pike — HUMAN · Ashenmere
- **Function:** Ashenmere's dockmaster; halfling-brewer friendly, goblin-courier tolerant; the town's unofficial ambassador.
- **Beat:** signs the Seam-moot's founding charter (the first human to sign a *nameless* law — the "small government" beat; a man of docks, signing a room).
- **Line:** *"I've signed shipping manifests for forty years. This one's got no name on it. That's the first time a document's ever* trusted *me."*
