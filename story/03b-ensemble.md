# 03b — TIER 3 · NAMED ENSEMBLE (283)

**What this file is:** the third ring of *named* characters — the recurring **named NPCs** who make each realm feel *lived-in*. Each entry has a **function** (a job/role in the realm) and a **beat** (a story function — the moment they matter, or the prejudice/theme they embody). None is a monster; each is a person with a job and a choice.

**Count:** **283** named ensemble characters (28 per realm × 9 realms + 28 cross-realm/caravan, plus 3 ratified by the manga as it was written — the *Ratified additions* below). Numbered 1–280, +281–283.

**How to use:** these are the *set-dressing with souls*. In manga/anime, each can be a background face that *earns a name* in a scene. In the TCG/PC games, each is a **named NPC / quest-giver / deck-flavor card**. In spin-offs, any of them can be promoted to a lead (the "world is the IP; the characters are the doors" rule).

## Realm 4 — Ulgar (28)

10. **Kha** — Orc, Ulrik's clan · young; Kharza's sister; the code's youngest student. **Ch. 29, APPOINTED:** at **eleven**, she is the realm's chosen **reader at the post at the edge** — named in the horn's copy, read aloud at the post's door nine hundred miles away, *student of the school in the well, youngest of the code* — because the realm's oldest rule about new law is **the newest word goes to the furthest door**. ### The minotaur / the troll / the cyclops

### The first cell’s camp at the edge of the world (Ch. 27–P28)

- **The camp** — new-timber stockade, gate, watchtower with a bell, tents in rows, kitchen, laundry, a well, and the **signboard in four scripts giving the hours of business**: the most frightening thing in Vol. 5 is a frontier outpost that is *civil*. Its officers are functions, not names (rule 7): the **gate-clerk** (clean coat, spectacles, immaculate, keeps the **gate-book**: *visitor · realm · purpose · sponsor*, with the company’s note against Ulgar under *disputed*), the **registrar** (cyclops, late middle age, the founder’s teacher’s apprentice, thirty years keeping the founder’s books, hears filings at **a table with four chairs and no bench**, and is the camp’s only truth-telling office), the **troll surgeon** (river-caste; the only safe approach to the wound in nine realms was built by a mender — he chisels the realm’s clause into chart-boards for a patient whose hands are wrapped), the **samplers** (grey-booted coats, dated sealed sample-boxes: the founder’s whole method is *samples or it did not happen*), the **old cyclops in the ward** (both hands wrapped, voice gone, asks to *hear* the reading, and turns his head toward it), and the founder’s **daughter** (**drawn, never named, never given a line about her father** — she walks past him in the yard for nine years and Ch. 28 is the only chapter in which he stops walking for one heartbeat).
- **The survey book** (Ch. 28 P6.1) — one hundred years of daily soundings on the wound, in the camp’s own hand, with the founder’s column ***what the grey did today***; the **names of the four** who read with him are in the front, and the realm now owns the whole copy. **The four who read with him** are paid emotionally and never named: two on the far shore of the grey, one in the ground at the far camp, one in his yard with a sample-box.
- **The fourth cell** (Ch. 28 P3.1, P10.1) — the off-sheet cell operating out of the mine-road east of the pass: no chart, no sample, no chain of custody; killed for the first time in the second spring nine years ago; **eleven dated sightings** in the founder’s file; four confirmed members; two captured and lost to a Silk court; and, three weeks before Ch. 28, four of them signed into the **old-forges** map-room as *pilgrims from the Rift*, asked for the holdings of the Barrens and the seam, **were given a reading, and left**. **Canon pattern: they take nothing anybody didn’t hand them.** They are the ones who killed Tarkhan (whoever took him off the road); they are named, and left **uncaptured**, and Morgatha has cut their road into her forearm, and the file is open.
- **The reading-post at the edge** (Ch. 28 P8.2, P12.1) — one room outside the camp gate: a chair, a lamp, a desk, a flag-pole with no flag, and a door cut in the realm’s own register: ***THE READING-POST AT THE EDGE. CLERK FIRST: DEEDS BEFORE DOORS.*** The terms (Viv’s five clauses) are **paid by the realm, one reader and one clerk, the survey’s books open, no alliance**, and the clause that matters: ***the reader may refuse.*** **The appointment is Ch. 29’s business**, and the chair is empty when the volume leaves the camp.
