# 05 — FUTURE SCOPE LIST (The Franchise Roadmap)

**The goal:** AURELION is not a story that *gets adapted* — it's a *world that gets built*. The saga is the *spine*; the scope is the *flesh*. Every medium below is a *different door* into the *same* world, and the canon spine (below) is what keeps them coherent.

---

## I. THE CANON SPINE (what to protect — the "fixed facts")

A franchise dies from *inconsistent canon*, not from *new* stories. The spine is the load-bearing structure every property depends on. **Do not break these:**

1. **The First Shattering** — Aurelion shattered itself; Vaelthorn is the negative image. (Fixed)
2. **The Nine Crowns** — nine shards, eight realms + the Seams (the Mosaic is the Ninth Crown). (Fixed)
3. **The Re-Oath** — a crown is *consent*, not force. (Fixed — this is the *theme*; breaking it breaks the IP)
4. **The Sigil system** — power costs *identity* (a memory, a future, a self). (Fixed)
5. **The Chord rule** — a Chord requires *two users who have chosen each other*. (Fixed)
6. **The Unraveling** — the real death is *not being you*; a name is the cure. (Fixed)
7. **The order of the Re-Oaths** — the Seams Re-Oath *first*. (Fixed)
8. **No race is the monster.** (Fixed — the *one* rule that makes 49 races feel real instead of decorative)

**Everything else is flexible.** New races, new realms, new cities, new characters, new villains — all *allowed*. The spine is the *bones*; the flesh is the *story*.

## V. CARD GAME (TCG / LCG) — *the system is the game*

**The design pillar:** the card game is not an *adaptation* of the power system — it *is* the power system. The Sigils, the Chords, the Unraveling, the Nine Crowns — the *story* is the *rules*, and the *rules* are the *story*.

### Core mechanics (the translation, from the power system)
| Story mechanic | Card-game rule | The theme it encodes |
|---|---|---|
| Sigil = soul-scar | Each card has a *Sigil mark* (its realm, its cost-shape) | "You are a piece of your world" |
| Resonance ranks | A card's *power* is gated by a *cost-shape* (a memory, a future, a self) | "Power costs identity" |
| Chord = two Sigils by choice | A *named combo* requires *two cards from different realms* + a *bond token* | "Unity is a choice, made by two" |
| The Unraveling | A *gray thread* counter that spreads across a player's cards | "The real death is not being you" |
| The Nine Crowns | Nine *artifact* cards (grief-vaults); a *Re-Oath* is a *consent ritual* | "A crown is consent, not force" |
| The Mosaic's cost | A *Mosaic card* can copy any Sigil, but *its own* Sigil frays (a *self-erosion* counter) | "The hero's power is his own erosion" |
| A name returned | A *Name-Return* card (undoes the Unraveling, for one) | "The cure is a name, kept" |

### The deck archetypes (the nine realms, nine playstyles)
| Realm | Archetype | The playstyle | The theme |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Terrania** (human) | **Hundredfold** | Cheap, combo-heavy, can *borrow* a keyword from any card on the field | "The any-race deck" |
| **Sylvaris** (elf) | **Everbloom** | Slow ramp, huge late-game, "century" tokens that persist across turns | "The long-game deck" |
| **Khazadûm** (dwarf) | **Everburn** | Tank/armor; "the mountain is a relative" = your permanents are a *family* (linked: one hurt, all defend) | "The family deck" |
| **Ulgar** (orc) | **Titanvein** | Spend *oaths* (permanent commitments) for power; break one, and the card *leaves your library* | "The oath deck" |
| **Thalassa** (mer) | **Tidesong** | "Chorus" tokens (multiple cards must resolve together to trigger); the song is a *combo you build with others* | "The chorus deck" |
| **Emberfall** (demon) | **Oathflame** | Cards that are *oaths* (commitments that trigger on conditions); break your own card's condition and it *burns* | "The legal deck" |
| **Seraphel** (angel) | **Seraphic** | High power, low cost, but *cannot be played with "impure" cards in the deck* (a deck-constraint) | "The purity deck" |
| **Nocturne** (dead) | **Veilward** | The *exchange* archetype: cards that *give* something to take something (a trade, not a drain) | "The trade deck" |
| **The Seams** (Mosaic) | **Nullsigil** | Cards that can *copy* a keyword from any other card on the field (the Mosaic mechanic); the *self-erosion* counter | "The hybrid deck" |

**The design rule:** the *deck is the theme*. A "Hundredfold" (human) deck is *cheap, combo-heavy, can borrow a keyword* (the any-race deck). A "Seraphic" (angel) deck is *high power, low cost, but cannot be played with "impure" cards* (the purity constraint). A "Titanvein" (orc) deck *spends oaths* (permanent commitments) for power; *break one, and the card leaves your library* (the theme is the mechanic). The game *is* the story.

## VI. PC GAMES (the interactive scope)

### Game 1 — *AURELION: The Mosaic War* (ARPG, the flagship)
- **The premise:** the S1–S3 story, *played* (the Mosaic Nine, the realm arcs, the Re-Oaths, the Hollow).
- **The class system:** 9 realms = 9 playable race/class families (human, elf, dwarf, orc, mer, demon, angel, dead, Mosaic). Each class has a *Sigil mark* and a *cost-shape* (the theme in the stat block).
- **The Mosaic class:** the hybrid build (the *Nullsigil* — can *borrow* a Sigil from any realm, but *erodes* itself; the *self-erosion* counter is the *visual* indicator of which realm's mechanics are active (the theme in the UI)).
- **The Chord system:** the *named combos* (the "special moves" of the saga) — two classes, a *bond*, a result (the theme in the *party*).
- **The Re-Oath system:** the *consent ritual* (a *multiplayer* act — the *whole* party must *choose* to Re-Oath, and the *consent* is the *power*, not the *artifact* (the theme in the *quest*)).
- **The Unraveling:** the *gray thread* counter (the *real* death is *not being you* — the *visual* is the *white-thread cracks* that run across the character's *Sigil mark* (the theme in the *character sheet*)).

### Game 2 — *Nine Realms* (4X strategy, the diplomatic scope)
- **The premise:** the *political* spine (the nine sovereigns, the Re-Oaths, the Council of Seams, the *consent* as a *game mechanic*).
- **The consent mechanic:** a *realm* can only be *Re-Oathed* if the *players* (the *sovereigns*) *choose* to *consent* — the *consent* is the *power*, not the *force* (the theme in the *diplomacy*).
- **The Reforgers:** the *cell-based* villains (each realm arc has a *cell*, and the *realm* defeats them *with its own law* — the *Ultravote*, the *Fire-Court*, the *chorus-trial* (the theme in the *law*).
- **The Unraveling:** the *gray thread* counter (the *real* threat is *not* the *Reforgers* — it's the *Unraveling* (the theme in the *strategy*).

### Game 3 — *AURELION: Sigils* (mobile, the *card game* on *mobile*)
- **The premise:** the TCG, *mobile* (the *card game* on *mobile* — the *Sigils*, the *Chords*, the *Unraveling*, the *Nine Crowns*).
- **The design rule:** the *mobile* is the *card game* (not a *new* game — the *card game* on *mobile*). The franchise math holds.

### Game 4 — *AURELION: The Seams* (MMO, the *long-term* scope)
- **The premise:** the *post-saga* world (the Council of Seams, the *healed* world, the *new* Mosaic-born, the *Seams* as the *busiest* place in the world).
- **The design rule:** the *MMO* is the *post-saga* (not the *saga* — the *after*). The *saga* is the *spine*; the *MMO* is the *flesh* (the *world* after the *story*). The franchise math holds.

## X. THE RISK LIST (what to avoid — the "don'ts")

1. **Don't make any race the monster.** (The *one* rule. Breaking it breaks the IP.)
2. **Don't break the spine.** (The *fixed facts* above. The *inconsistent canon* is the *franchise killer*.)
3. **Don't let the power escalate past the theme.** (The *Unraveling* is the *ceiling*; the *cost* is the *point*. A *power* that doesn't *cost identity* is a *power* that *breaks the theme*.)
4. **Don't let the Chord be a "command."** (A Chord requires *two users who have chosen each other* — a *bond*, not a *command*. A Chord that is a *command* is a Chord that *breaks the theme*.)
5. **Don't let the Re-Oath be a "force."** (A crown is *consent*, not force. A Re-Oath that is a *force* is a Re-Oath that *breaks the theme*.)
6. **Don't let the villain win.** (The *ending* is the *institute* (the Council of Seams). A *villain* who *wins* is a *villain* who *breaks the spine*.)
7. **Don't let the sequel be a "reset."** (The *sequel* is the *same world, one realm further out*. A *reset* is a *sequel* that *breaks the spine*.)

**The risk's design rule:** the *spine* is the *bones*; the *flesh* is the *story*. The *bones* don't *break*. The *flesh* *grows*. The franchise math holds.
