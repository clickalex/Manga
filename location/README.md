# Locations

> Where the story lives. This folder holds the *world* side of the IP: the places the reader can be dropped into, drawn, or played in. Story canon (what happens in a place) lives in [../story/](../story/README.md); character design (who lives there) lives in [../character/](../character/README.md).

## Files here

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [ashenmere.md](ashenmere.md) | **Ashenmere** — the pilot town: the reader's map (in reading order), the art team's design guide (the two rooflines, the Veil-water, the weather-as-character rule), the Festival of Open Doors (the Seams' Re-Oath in miniature), and what the town is *for* (the franchise's front door). |
| [nine-realms.md](nine-realms.md) | **The Nine Realms** — the world guide: each realm's location notes (culture on the page, colors, first pilot appearance), the Nine-Color Key, the geography rule (the Veil connects everything; the Seams are the center; every realm has a door and a law), and Realm 10 (reserved — "the tenth, quiet, hungry"). |

## Rules

- **Every place gets: a face, a door, a law, a color.** (Face = the people; door = the physical threshold; law = the procedure that rules it — the grove-council vote, the Ultravote, the Moot's room; color = its entry in the Nine-Color Key, [../character/mosaic-nine.md](../character/mosaic-nine.md).) A location without a law is a backdrop; a location with a law is a *story* — and the Reforgers lose at every door by the door's *own* law (canon design rule, [../story/03-characters.md](../story/03-characters.md)).
- **Locations grow from the story up.** If a chapter names a new place, it gets a line here *after* it's on the page — the page is the canon, this file is the index.
- **The map is Fenn's.** Any map graphic in any medium is drawn from his (the nine-pointed crown, the Seams at the center in patchwork, the gray stains at the edges — [../story/manga/chapter-01.md](../story/manga/chapter-01.md) P12.1), and the blank edge (Realm 10) is always there. The world is *unfinished*, and only one person will admit it.
